Carnivores use their sharp nails to attack their prey. They use their sharp, pointed teeth to bite and tear their prey.

Observe the butterflies in your surroundings. Find their names and write them. Butterflies depend on plants in two ways; for laying eggs and for food. The Common mormon butterfly (Naarakakkali) usually lays eggs on the citrus plant (naarakam) and the orangeberry plant (paanal). Their larvae eat the leaves of these plants. When they become butterflies, they find nectar from the flowers of hibiscus, white orchid tree and morning breeze plants. 34
