The Sun

The Sun is also a star. It is the star closest to the Earth.

We cannot see the stars during the day because of the light of the Sun.

It is the light from the Sun that reaches the Earth. Haven’t you seen the Sun at sunrise and sunset? In which shape do you see the Sun? The Sun is a very large sphere compared to the Earth. Since it is so far away the Sun appears small to us when we look at it from the Earth.

Let’s observe the Moon

The Moon, which is the celestial sphere closest to the Earth, cannot shine by itself. The Moon reflects the Sun’s light falling on it. This is the reason for the Moon’s glow and the moonlight at night. So it is the Sun itself that gives the light at night!
