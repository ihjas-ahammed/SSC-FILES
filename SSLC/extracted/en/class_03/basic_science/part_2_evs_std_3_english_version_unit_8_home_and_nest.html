Home and Nest The weaver bird mates (Attakkuruvi) had a wish

They wanted to build a nest before the rainy season. The birds claimed a tree branch.

The male weaver bird started building the nest… A beautiful nest was made within days using fibres pecked from paddy and coconut leaves. Then the female laid eggs and they waited for the little ones to come out. The eggs hatched and the baby birds came out. Days passed. The baby birds were ready to fly.

The weaver birds and their young ones flew far away.
