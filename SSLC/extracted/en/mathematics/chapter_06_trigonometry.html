Numbers computed like these have special names. In the problem just seen, the number 0.6428... shows what fraction of the distance between a point on one side of a 40° angle and the vertex of the angle, is the height of the perpendicular from this point to the other side. This number is called the sine of 40° and is written sin 40°. Thus sin 40° ≈ 0.6428 The second number 0.7660... shows what fraction of the distance between a point on one side of a 40° angle and the vertex of the angle, is the distance between the foot of the perpendicular from this point to the other side and the vertex of the angle. This number is called the cosine of 40° and is written cos 40°. Thus cos 40° ≈ 0.7660
