The sides of the triangle are formed by the tangents to the incircle from the vertices. And the lengths of the tangents from each corner are equal. We have noted that the area of a triangle with length of sides a, b, c is s(s − a) (s − b) (s − c) . As an example, lets compute the area of a triangle of sides 12, 35 and 43 centimetres. Taking a = 12, b = 35, c = 43 centimetres we get s =

12 + 35 + 43 = 45, 2

s − a = 33, s − b = 10, s − c = 2. Using a calculator, we can compute the area. Area =

45 × 33 × 10 × 2 ≈ 172.33 square centimetres
