The square of any non-multiple of 3 leaves remainder 1 on division by 3. The square of any multiple of 3 is again a multiple of 3, right ? (Why?) So, we have this general result Any perfect square leaves remainder 0 or 1 on division by 3. This can also be stated like this: Any number which leaves remainder 2 on division by 3 is not a perfect square.
