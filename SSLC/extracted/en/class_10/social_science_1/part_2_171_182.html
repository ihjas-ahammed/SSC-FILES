Population Population is an indispensable factor in the formation of a state. For the existence of a state, the people should live unitedly with mutual understanding, inter dependence and common public interest. There is no state without people. However, the minimum and the maximum number of people for a country are not fixed. Population which is too much or too less is not good for a state. These two aspects will adversely affect the development of a state. States like Canada with less population encourages migration. Scarcity of human resource is the reason for this.
