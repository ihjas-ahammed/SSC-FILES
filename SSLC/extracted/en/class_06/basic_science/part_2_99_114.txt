The difference in the visibility of the lighted and dark areas of the moon when observed
from the earth is the reason for waxing and waning of the moon. From the new moon
day to the full moon day, the lighted portion of the moon becomes more visible. This
is called waxing. From the full moon day to the new moon day, there is a decrease in
the visibility of the lighted area of moon from the earth. This is called waning.

complete one rotation by the time the moon completes one revolution?
