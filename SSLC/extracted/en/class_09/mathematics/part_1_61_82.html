Special operations What we write as algebraic identities are general principles on numbers and their operations. Using them, we can prove some other general results also. For example, We can see that the product of two odd numbers is odd by inspecting some pairs of odd numbers. How do we actually prove this for any two odd numbers? We’ve seen in Class 7 that the general form of an odd number is 2n + 1, n = 0, 1, 2, 3,... So we can take two odd numbers in general as 2m + 1 and 2n + 1.
