fractional multiple of the length of its side. In other words, if we take the side of a square

lengths like these and new numbers to denote them. (We will see another such number
in the lesson, Circular Measures).
Such numbers which are used to denote lenghts which cannot be expressed as fractions
of a unit are called irrational numbers.
We have also seen how joining lengths denoted by irrational numbers leads to the
definition of addition of such numbers. We now see how computation of areas lead to
