Question 8.
Ethanol is an alcohol which is extensively used for industrial purposes.
(a) What is rectified spirit? (1)
(b) How does rectified spirit differ from absolute alcohol? (1)
Answer:
(a) Rectified spirit is 95.6% pure ethanol. It is obtained by the fractional distillation of wash.
(b) Absolute alcohol is 99.5% pure ethanol. It is obtained by the fractional distillation of rectified spirit.

Question 19.
The reversible reaction at equilibrium is given.
2NO (g) + O2 (g) \(\rightleftharpoons\) 2NO2 (g) + Heat
Answer the following:
(a) How does an increase in temperature influence the amount of product formed? (1)
(b) What change is to be made in pressure to get more product? (1)
(c) How does an increase in concentration of oxygen influence the rate of forward reaction? (1)
(d) What is the function of a catalyst in reversible reactions? (1)
Answer:
(a) When temperature increases the amount of product decreases. Because the forward reaction is exothermic.
(b) Due to forward reaction number of molecules decreases. So to get more product pressure should be increased.
(c) When the concentration of oxygen increases the amount of product increases.
(d) Catalyst speeds up both forward and backward reactions. So equilibrium is attained earlier.
