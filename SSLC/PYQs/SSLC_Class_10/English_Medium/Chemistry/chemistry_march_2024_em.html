Question 1.

Question 2.

(Levigation, Froth Floatation, Magnetic Separation, Leaching)

Froth Floatation

Question 7.
(a) The oxidation state of Mn in MnO2 is 4. Find out the oxidation state of Mn in Mn2O3.
(Hint: Oxidation state of oxygen = -2)
(b) Transition elements show variable oxidation state. Why?
Answer:
(a) Mn2O3
2x – 6 = 0 ⇒ x = +3
(b) In the case of transition elements, along with outermost s electrons, the d electrons from the penultimate shell are also lost, as the energies of these subshells are almost equal.

Section – C

Answer any 4 questions from 11 to 15. Each question carries 3 scores. (4 × 3 = 12)

Question 11.
Sulphuric acid is called the king of chemicals.
(a) Industrial preparation of sulphuric acid is known as _______________
(b) Which is the catalyst used in this process?
(c) A black substance is formed When concentrated sulphuric acid is added to sugar. Explain the reason for this observation.
Answer:
(a) Contact Process
(b) Vanadium Pentoxide (V2O5)
(c) Dehydrating nature of sulphuric acid.
Sulphuric acid absorbs hydrogen and oxygen in the form of water from sugar (C12H22O11).

Answer:
(a) Copper
(b) Silver Nitrate (AgNO3) / Sodium Cyanide + Silver Cyanide Solution
(c) Ag → Ag+ + 1e–

Question 15.
Complete the following equations.
(a) X + Cl2 → CH3 – CH2Cl + HCl
(b) CH3 – CH = CH2 + Cl2 → Y
(c) CH3 – CH2 – CH3 \(\rightarrow{\text { Heat }}\) CH4 + Z
Answer:
(a) X : CH3 – CH3
CH3 – CH3 + Cl2 → CH3 – CH2Cl + HCl
(b) Y : CH3 – CHCl – CH2Cl
CH3 – CH = CH2 + Cl2 → CH3 – CHCl – CH2Cl
(c) Z : CH2 = CH2
CH3 – CH2 – CH3 \(\rightarrow{\text { Heat }}\) CH4 + CH2 = CH2

Question 18.
(a) What is the volume of 1 mol ammonia gas (NH3) at STP?
(b) Find the volume of 68 g ammonia gas at STP.
[Hint: Molecular Mass of NH3 = 17]
(c) Find the number of molecules present in 68 g ammonia gas.
Answer:
(a) 22.4 L
(b) No. of moles \(=\frac{\text { Given mass }}{\text { GMM }}\)
= \(\frac{68}{17}\)
= 4 mole
Volume = Mole × 22.4
= 4 × 22.4
= 89.6 E
(c) No. of molecules = Mole × NA
= 4NA (4 × 6.022 × 1023)

Question 19.
2NO (g) + O2 (g) = 2NO2 (g) + Heat
(a) When does a reversible reaction attain equilibrium?
(b) How do the following changes influence the amount of the product?
(i) Increase in temperature
(ii) Increase in pressure
(iii) NO2 is removed from the system.
Answer:
(a) The system attains equilibrium when the rate of forward reaction becomes equal to the rate of backward reaction.
(b) (i) The amount of product decreases.
(ii) the amount of product increases.
(iii) amount of product increases.

Question 20.
The order of reactivity of some elements is given. Analyze it and answer the following questions.
Mg > Zn > Fe > Cu > Ag
(a) Name any one metal that cannot displace hydrogen from dilute hydrochloric acid.
(b) Which are the metals that can displace Fe from the FeSO4 solution?
(c) A galvanic cell is constructed using Zn and Fe as electrodes.
(i) Which is the anode of this cell?
(ii) Write the equation of the chemical reaction taking place at the cathode.
Answer:
(a) Copper (Cu) or Silver (Ag)
(b) Mg and Zn
(c) (i) Zn
(ii) Fe2+ + 2e– → Fe
