# Degeneracy

**One-line meaning:** Several independent eigenvectors share the same eigenvalue.

**Formal definition:** An [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalue]] is degenerate if its [[Eigenspace|eigenspace]], the [[Set|set]] of its [[Eigenvector|eigenvectors]] together with zero, has [[Dimension|dimension]] greater than 1.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 4 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $A=2I$ on $\mathbb C^2$ has eigenvalue 2 with degeneracy 2.
