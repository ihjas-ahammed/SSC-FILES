# Hilbert Space

**One-line meaning:** A vector space with an overlap rule and no missing limits measured by that rule.

**Formal definition:** A Hilbert space is an [[Inner Product|inner-product]] [[Vector Space|vector space]] complete in the induced [[Norm|norm]] $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v|v\rangle}$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\mathbb C^2$ with its usual inner product is a Hilbert space.
