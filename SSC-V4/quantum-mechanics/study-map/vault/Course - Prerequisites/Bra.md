# Bra

**One-line meaning:** The rule associated with a ket that takes another ket and returns their overlap.

**Formal definition:** The bra $\langle\psi|$ maps $|\phi\rangle$ to $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle$. For [[Coordinate|coordinates]] $\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}$, its row is $(a^*,b^*)$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 28 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The bra of $\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}$ is $(1,-i)$.
