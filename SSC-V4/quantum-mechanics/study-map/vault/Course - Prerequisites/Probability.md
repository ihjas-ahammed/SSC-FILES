# Probability

**One-line meaning:** A number from zero to one measuring how likely an outcome is.

**Formal definition:** For an event, a [[Set|set]] of outcomes, $0\le P(E)\le1$. The entire outcome [[Set|set]] has probability 1. Probabilities of disjoint events add; disjoint means sharing no outcome.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 18 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** A fair two-outcome experiment gives probability $1/2$ for either result.
