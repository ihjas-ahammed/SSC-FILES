# Complex Conjugate

**One-line meaning:** A complex number with the sign of its imaginary part reversed.

**Formal definition:** If $z=a+ib$, then $z^*=a-ib$; $(zw)^*=z^*w^*$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 30 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(2+3i)^*=2-3i$.
