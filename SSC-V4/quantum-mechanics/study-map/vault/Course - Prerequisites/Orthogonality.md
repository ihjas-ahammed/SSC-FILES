# Orthogonality

**One-line meaning:** Two vectors have zero overlap.

**Formal definition:** $u$ and $v$ are orthogonal when $\langle u|v\rangle=0$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 19 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ are orthogonal.
