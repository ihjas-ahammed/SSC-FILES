# Anticommutator

**One-line meaning:** The sum of two operator products in opposite orders.

**Formal definition:** $\{A,B\}=AB+BA$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\{A,A\}=2A^2$.
