# Secular Equation

**One-line meaning:** The equation whose roots are matrix eigenvalues.

**Formal definition:** $A v=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$ requires $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$, $(2-\lambda)(3-\lambda)=0$.
