# Trace

**One-line meaning:** The sum of a square matrix’s diagonal entries.

**Formal definition:** $\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_i A_{ii}$. For finite square matrices, $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$, and the trace equals the sum of [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] counted with [[Multiplicity|multiplicity]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The trace of $\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$ is $5$.
