# Field

**One-line meaning:** A collection of numbers where addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division by nonzero numbers work.

**Formal definition:** A field is a [[Set|set]] with [[Basic Algebra|addition]] and multiplication, [[Associativity|associative]] and [[Commutativity|commutative]], with $0$, $1\ne0$, additive inverses, multiplicative inverses for nonzero elements, and $a(b+c)=ab+ac$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\mathbb R$ and $\mathbb C$ are fields; integers fail because $1/2$ is not an integer.
