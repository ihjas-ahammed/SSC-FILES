# Span

**One-line meaning:** All vectors you can make from a given collection.

**Formal definition:** The span of $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ is the [[Set|set]] of all their linear combinations.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 19 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The span of $(1,0)$ is the horizontal line.
