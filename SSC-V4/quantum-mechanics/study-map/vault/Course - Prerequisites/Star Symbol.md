# Star Symbol

**One-line meaning:** A raised star on a number means replace i by −i.

**Formal definition:** $z^*$ is the [[Complex Conjugate]] of the [[Scalar]] $z$. Unlike $A^\dagger$, entrywise $A^*$ does not [[Transpose|transpose]] a [[Matrix]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 9 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(2+3i)^*=2-3i$.
