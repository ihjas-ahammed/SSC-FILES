# Completeness Relation

**One-line meaning:** Adding all basis-component projections reconstructs every vector.

**Formal definition:** For a complete [[Orthonormal Basis|orthonormal basis]], $\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|=I$ and $|\psi\rangle=\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|\psi\rangle$. Infinite sums converge in the Hilbert-[[Mathematical Space|space]] [[Norm|norm]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 11 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** In two dimensions $|1\rangle\langle1|+|2\rangle\langle2|=I$.
