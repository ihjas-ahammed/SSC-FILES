# Index Notation

**One-line meaning:** Small labels identify entries or members of a numbered collection.

**Formal definition:** In a [[Matrix]] entry $A_{ij}$, the [[Integer]] $i$ labels the row and $j$ the column. In $c_j$, $j$ labels a [[Coordinate]]. A superscript may denote a power or a named operation, so read context.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 24 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $A_{12}$ is the entry in row 1, column 2.
