# Generalized State

**One-line meaning:** An idealized state symbol interpreted through its action on suitable functions.

**Formal definition:** [[Continuity|Continuous]]-[[Basis|basis]] kets such as $|x\rangle$ or $|p\rangle$ are generalized states. They are described distributionally, and need not belong to the ordinary [[Hilbert Space|Hilbert space]] of [[Normalization|normalizable]] states.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 10 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Exact position states obey delta normalization.
