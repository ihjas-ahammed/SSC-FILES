# Continuous Basis

**One-line meaning:** A continuously labelled family of generalized state symbols.

**Formal definition:** Position states obey $\langle x|x\prime\rangle=\delta(x-x\prime)$ and $\int|x\rangle\langle x|dx=I$; momentum states obey the corresponding $p$ relations. These are [[Generalized State|generalized states]], not [[Normalization|normalizable]] [[Vector|vectors]] of $L^2$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 9 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** A physical wavepacket can be expanded as $|\psi\rangle=\int\psi(x)|x\rangle dx$.
