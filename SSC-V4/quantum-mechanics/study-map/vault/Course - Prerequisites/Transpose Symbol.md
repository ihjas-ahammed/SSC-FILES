# Transpose Symbol

**One-line meaning:** A raised T swaps the row and column positions of a matrix.

**Formal definition:** $(A^T)_{ij}=A_{ji}$ defines the [[Transpose]]. A transpose alone does not take a [[Complex Conjugate]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}^{T}=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$.
