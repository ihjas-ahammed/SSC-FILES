# Complete Space

**One-line meaning:** A space with no missing limits of Cauchy sequences.

**Formal definition:** A [[Normed Space|normed space]] is complete when every [[Cauchy Sequence|Cauchy sequence]] in it converges to an element of the same [[Mathematical Space|space]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Rational numbers are incomplete: rational approximations can approach $\sqrt2$, which is not rational.
