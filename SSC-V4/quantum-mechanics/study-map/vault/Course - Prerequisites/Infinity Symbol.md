# Infinity Symbol

**One-line meaning:** ∞ means a quantity grows without a finite bound; it is not a real number.

**Formal definition:** $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx$ denotes an [[Integral]] over the whole [[Real Number|real]] line, defined by appropriate [[Limit|limits]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 5 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\int_0^\infty e^{-x}\,dx=1$.
