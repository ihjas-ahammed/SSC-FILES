# Conjugate Symmetry

**One-line meaning:** Swapping the two vectors conjugates their overlap.

**Formal definition:** $\langle u|v\rangle=\langle v|u\rangle^*$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** If $\langle u|v\rangle=2+i$, then $\langle v|u\rangle=2-i$.
