# Dagger Symbol

**One-line meaning:** The raised dagger means take the adjoint: transpose and complex-conjugate a matrix.

**Formal definition:** $A^\dagger$ denotes the [[Hermitian Adjoint]] of $A$. For a [[Matrix]], $(A^\dagger)_{ij}=A_{ji}^*$. For a [[Ket]], $|\psi\rangle^\dagger=\langle\psi|$, its [[Bra]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 7 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\begin{pmatrix}1&i\end{pmatrix}^{\dagger}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix}$.
