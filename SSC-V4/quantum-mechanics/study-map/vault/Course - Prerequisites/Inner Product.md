# Inner Product

**One-line meaning:** A rule that pairs two vectors and returns a number measuring their overlap.

**Formal definition:** Using the physics convention, $\langle u|av+bw\rangle=a\langle u|v\rangle+b\langle u|w\rangle$, $\langle u|v\rangle=\langle v|u\rangle^*$, and $\langle v|v\rangle\ge0$, with equality only for $v=0$. In $\mathbb C^n$, $\langle u|v\rangle=\sum_i u_i^*v_i$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 30 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $u=(1,i)$, $v=(2,1)$, $\langle u|v\rangle=2-i$.
