# Equality and Inequality Signs

**One-line meaning:** These signs say whether values are equal or one is bounded by another.

**Formal definition:** $a=b$ means equal; $a\ne b$ means unequal; $a\le b$ means no greater than; $a\ge b$ means no smaller than. These are statements in [[Basic Algebra]], not [[Linear Operator|operators]] on states.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $3\ge2$ and $1\ne0$ are true.
