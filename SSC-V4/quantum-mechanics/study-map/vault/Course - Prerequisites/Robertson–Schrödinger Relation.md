# Robertson–Schrödinger Relation

**One-line meaning:** A stronger uncertainty bound that also includes symmetric correlation.

**Formal definition:** $(\Delta A)^2(\Delta B)^2\ge\tfrac14|\langle[A,B]\rangle|^2+\operatorname{Cov}(A,B)^2$. Dropping the nonnegative [[Covariance|covariance]] term gives the Robertson bound.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** A nonzero covariance strengthens the lower bound.
