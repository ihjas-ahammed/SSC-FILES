# Schwarz Inequality

**One-line meaning:** The overlap of two vectors cannot exceed the product of their lengths.

**Formal definition:** $|\langle u|v\rangle|^2\le\langle u|u\rangle\langle v|v\rangle$. Equality holds exactly for [[Linear Independence|linearly dependent]] [[Vector|vectors]], including the zero-[[Vector|vector]] cases.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 4 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For orthogonal unit vectors, $0\le1$.
