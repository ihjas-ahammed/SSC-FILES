# Almost Everywhere

**One-line meaning:** A statement may fail at points with zero total length and still hold for the purposes of integration.

**Formal definition:** Two [[Function|functions]] agree almost everywhere when the [[Set]] where they differ is a [[Negligible Set]]. In $L^2$, such functions represent the same [[Square-Integrable Function]] state.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Changing a function at $x=0$ alone does not change its squared integral.
