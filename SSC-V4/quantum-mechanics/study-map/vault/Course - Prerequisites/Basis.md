# Basis

**One-line meaning:** An independent collection from which every vector can be built.

**Formal definition:** A basis is a linearly independent [[Set|set]] spanning the [[Vector Space|vector space]]; in finite dimensions every [[Vector|vector]] has a unique expansion in it.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 19 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $e_1=(1,0)$ and $e_2=(0,1)$ form a basis of $\mathbb R^2$.
