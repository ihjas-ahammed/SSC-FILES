# Simultaneous Eigenvectors

**One-line meaning:** Vectors that are eigenvectors of both operators.

**Formal definition:** [[Dimension|Finite-dimensional]] [[Commutator|commuting]] [[Hermitian Operator|Hermitian]] operators admit a common [[Orthonormal Basis|orthonormal eigenbasis]]. A nondegenerate [[Eigenvector|eigenvector]] of $A$ is also an [[Eigenvector|eigenvector]] of $B$; within a [[Degeneracy|degenerate]] [[Eigenspace|eigenspace]] choose a [[Basis|basis]] diagonalizing $B$. Commutation alone does not give this result for arbitrary defective operators.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Two diagonal matrices share the coordinate basis.
