# Complex Number Arithmetic

**One-line meaning:** Arithmetic with numbers of the form $a+ib$, where $i^2=-1$.

**Formal definition:** For real $a,b$, add and multiply $a+ib$ using ordinary algebra and $i^2=-1$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(1+i)(1-i)=2$.
