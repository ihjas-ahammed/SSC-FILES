# Continuity

**One-line meaning:** Small input changes near a point produce arbitrarily small output changes.

**Formal definition:** A real or complex [[Function|function]] is continuous at $x$ when for every $\epsilon>0$ some $\delta>0$ makes $|f(y)-f(x)|<\epsilon$ whenever $|y-x|<\delta$. More general [[Mathematical Space|spaces]] use their specified notions of closeness.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 10 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $f(x)=x^2$ is continuous at every real x.
