# Fourier Transform

**One-line meaning:** A change from a function’s position description to its momentum description.

**Formal definition:** $\phi(p)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{-ipx/\hbar}\psi(x)dx$ and $\psi(x)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{ipx/\hbar}\phi(p)dp$. With this convention it preserves the [[Norm|norm]]; integrals are understood in the $L^2$ sense when needed.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 5 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** A sharply localized position packet needs a broad spread of momentum components.
