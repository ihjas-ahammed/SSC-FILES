# Norm

**One-line meaning:** The length of a vector.

**Formal definition:** The norm induced by an [[Inner Product|inner product]] is $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v|v\rangle}$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 30 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\|(3,4)\|=5$.
