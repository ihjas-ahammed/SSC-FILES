# Question
Write the matrix representation of the ket |ψ⟩ = a |1⟩ + b |2⟩, in the basis {|1⟩, |2⟩}.

# Formal Answer
1.
In the stated ordered [[Basis|basis]], the coefficients of $|1\rangle$ and $|2\rangle$ are $a$ and $b$.

2.
Thus the [[Matrix Representation|matrix representation]] is $|\psi\rangle\leftrightarrow\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}$.
The [[Ket|ket]] uses the coefficients themselves, not their [[Complex Conjugate|complex conjugates]].
