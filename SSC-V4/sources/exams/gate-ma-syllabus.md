# GATE MA — Mathematics

*Source: official GATE 2027 MA syllabus (IIT Madras). Paper pattern: 65 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours. General Aptitude 15 marks + Mathematics 85 marks. Negative marking applies to MCQs only (1/3 for 1-mark, 2/3 for 2-mark); MSQ and NAT carry no negative marking.*

## Part 1: General Aptitude - 15 marks

*Common to every GATE paper. Typically 5 one-mark and 5 two-mark questions.*

### General Aptitude - 15 marks

#### Verbal Aptitude {ga.verb}
- [ ] **English grammar** Tenses, articles, adjectives, prepositions, conjunctions, verb-noun agreement, and other parts of speech.
  - Subject-verb agreement and common concord traps
  - Correct use of articles and prepositions
- [ ] **Basic vocabulary** Words, idioms and phrases in context.
  - Synonyms and antonyms in context
  - Idioms and phrasal verbs
- [ ] **Reading and comprehension** Understanding a passage and answering inference questions.
  - Main idea, tone and author's intent
  - Inference vs. stated fact
- [ ] **Narrative sequencing** Arranging jumbled sentences into a coherent paragraph.
  - Identifying the opening sentence and connectors

#### Quantitative Aptitude {ga.quant}
- [ ] **Data interpretation** Data graphs (bar, pie, line, and other graphs representing data), 2- and 3-dimensional plots, maps and tables.
  - Reading percentages and ratios off charts
  - Multi-chart comparison questions
- [ ] **Numerical computation and estimation** Ratios, percentages, powers, exponents and logarithms, permutations and combinations, series.
  - Percentage change and successive percentages
  - Permutations, combinations and simple series
- [ ] **Mensuration and geometry** Areas, volumes, and basic coordinate geometry.
  - Areas and volumes of standard solids
- [ ] **Elementary statistics and probability** Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, and basic probability.
  - Weighted averages
  - Simple probability and expected value

#### Analytical Aptitude {ga.anly}
- [ ] **Logic** Deduction and induction.
  - Valid vs. invalid deductive arguments
- [ ] **Analogy and word groups** Identifying relationships between pairs.
- [ ] **Numerical relations and reasoning** Puzzles, arrangements, blood relations, directions.
  - Seating and linear arrangements
  - Coding-decoding

#### Spatial Aptitude {ga.spat}
- [ ] **Transformation of shapes** Translation, rotation, scaling, mirroring, assembling and grouping.
  - Mirror and water images
- [ ] **Paper folding, cutting and patterns** Patterns in 2 and 3 dimensions.
  - Paper folding and punching
  - Cube folding and dice problems

## Part 2: Mathematics - 85 marks

*Eleven sections. All sections are compulsory — there are no optional sections in the MA paper.*

### Section 1: Calculus {ma.calc}
- [ ] **Functions of two or more variables** Continuity, directional derivatives, partial derivatives, total derivative.
  - Continuity vs. existence of partial derivatives
  - Differentiability and the total derivative
- [ ] **Taylor's theorem, maxima and minima** Saddle points and the second derivative test in several variables.
  - Hessian and classification of critical points
- [ ] **Method of Lagrange's multipliers** Constrained optimisation in several variables.
- [ ] **Double and triple integrals** Jacobians and change of variables.
  - Change of order of integration
  - Polar, cylindrical and spherical substitutions
- [ ] **Applications to area, volume and surface area** Computing geometric quantities with multiple integrals.
- [ ] **Vector calculus** Gradient, divergence and curl; line integrals and surface integrals.
  - Conservative fields and path independence
- [ ] **Integral theorems** Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem and the Gauss divergence theorem.
  - Choosing the right theorem for a given integral

### Section 2: Linear Algebra {ma.la}
- [ ] **Finite dimensional vector spaces** Vector spaces over real or complex fields, basis and dimension.
  - Subspaces, sums and direct sums
- [ ] **Linear transformations** Matrix representations, rank and nullity.
  - Rank-nullity theorem
  - Change of basis and similarity
- [ ] **Systems of linear equations** Consistency, solution spaces and row reduction.
- [ ] **Characteristic polynomial, eigenvalues and eigenvectors** Algebraic and geometric multiplicity.
- [ ] **Diagonalization, minimal polynomial, Cayley-Hamilton theorem** Conditions for diagonalizability.
  - Minimal polynomial vs. characteristic polynomial
- [ ] **Finite dimensional inner product spaces** Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization process.
  - Orthogonal complements and projections
- [ ] **Special classes of matrices** Symmetric, skew-symmetric, Hermitian, skew-Hermitian, normal, orthogonal and unitary matrices; diagonalization by a unitary matrix.
  - Spectral properties of normal matrices
- [ ] **Jordan canonical form** Computing the Jordan form from elementary divisors.
- [ ] **Bilinear and quadratic forms** Signature, rank and Sylvester's law of inertia.

### Section 3: Real Analysis {ma.ra}
- [ ] **Metric spaces** Open and closed sets, convergence, limit points.
- [ ] **Completeness and Baire category theorem** Complete metric spaces and their consequences.
- [ ] **Contraction mapping principle** Banach fixed point theorem and applications.
- [ ] **Connectedness and compactness** Equivalent characterisations in metric spaces.
  - Heine-Borel and sequential compactness
- [ ] **Continuity and uniform continuity** Of functions between metric spaces.
- [ ] **Sequences and series of functions** Uniform convergence; Ascoli-Arzela theorem.
  - Uniform convergence and interchange of limits
  - Equicontinuity
- [ ] **Weierstrass approximation theorem** Density of polynomials in C[a,b].
- [ ] **Power series** Radius of convergence and term-wise operations.
- [ ] **Differentiation of functions of several variables** Inverse and implicit function theorems.
- [ ] **Lebesgue measure on the real line** Measurable sets and measurable functions.
  - Outer measure and Caratheodory criterion
- [ ] **Lebesgue integral and convergence theorems** Fatou's lemma, monotone convergence theorem, dominated convergence theorem.
- [ ] **Lp spaces** Holder and Minkowski inequalities, completeness.

### Section 4: Complex Analysis {ma.ca}
- [ ] **Functions of a complex variable** Continuity, differentiability, analytic functions, harmonic functions.
  - Cauchy-Riemann equations
  - Harmonic conjugates
- [ ] **Complex integration** Cauchy's integral theorem and Cauchy's integral formula.
- [ ] **Liouville's theorem, maximum modulus principle, Morera's theorem** Consequences of analyticity.
- [ ] **Zeros and singularities** Removable, pole and essential singularities.
  - Classifying singularities from the Laurent expansion
- [ ] **Power series, Taylor's and Laurent's series** Radius of convergence.
- [ ] **Residue theorem and applications** Evaluating real integrals by residues.
  - Contour choice for improper real integrals
- [ ] **Rouche's theorem, argument principle, Schwarz lemma** Counting zeros and mapping bounds.
- [ ] **Conformal mappings and Mobius transformations** Cross ratio and mapping of standard regions.

### Section 5: Ordinary Differential Equations {ma.ode}
- [ ] **First order ordinary differential equations** Exact, separable, linear and reducible forms.
- [ ] **Existence and uniqueness theorems for initial value problems** Picard-Lindelof and Peano.
  - Lipschitz condition and its role
- [ ] **Linear ODEs of higher order with constant coefficients** Complementary function and particular integral.
- [ ] **Second order linear ODEs with variable coefficients** Reduction of order and variation of parameters.
- [ ] **Cauchy-Euler equation** Equidimensional equations and their solutions.
- [ ] **Laplace transforms** Definition, basic properties and application to solving ODEs.
  - Convolution theorem and shifting theorems
- [ ] **Series solutions** Power series method and the Frobenius method.
  - Ordinary, regular singular and irregular singular points
- [ ] **Legendre and Bessel functions** Orthogonality properties and generating functions.
- [ ] **Systems of linear first order ODEs** Matrix exponential and fundamental matrices.
- [ ] **Sturm's oscillation and separation theorems** Zeros of solutions of second order equations.
- [ ] **Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue problems** Eigenvalues, eigenfunctions and orthogonality.
- [ ] **Planar autonomous systems** Stability of stationary points for linear systems with constant coefficients, linearized stability, Lyapunov functions.
  - Phase portraits: node, saddle, focus, centre

### Section 6: Algebra {ma.alg}
- [ ] **Groups, subgroups, normal subgroups, quotient groups** Cosets and Lagrange's theorem.
- [ ] **Homomorphisms and automorphisms** Isomorphism theorems.
- [ ] **Cyclic groups and permutation groups** Order of elements, cycle structure, alternating groups.
- [ ] **Group action** Orbit-stabilizer theorem and class equation.
- [ ] **Finite abelian groups** Structure theorem and invariant factors.
- [ ] **Sylow's theorems and their applications** Existence, conjugacy and counting of Sylow subgroups.
  - Proving non-simplicity of groups of given order
- [ ] **Rings and ideals** Prime and maximal ideals, quotient rings.
- [ ] **Unique factorization domains, principal ideal domains, Euclidean domains** The chain of inclusions and counterexamples.
- [ ] **Polynomial rings and Eisenstein's irreducibility criterion** Irreducibility tests over Q and finite fields.
- [ ] **Fields and field extensions** Algebraic extensions, degree of an extension, algebraically closed fields.
- [ ] **Finite fields** Structure, existence and uniqueness, multiplicative group.

### Section 7: Functional Analysis {ma.fa}
- [ ] **Normed linear spaces and Banach spaces** Completeness and equivalence of norms.
- [ ] **Separability** Separable and non-separable classical spaces.
- [ ] **Bounded linear operators and compact linear operators** Operator norm and basic properties.
- [ ] **Dual spaces and the Hahn-Banach theorem** Extension and separation forms.
- [ ] **Open mapping and closed graph theorems** Statements and standard applications.
- [ ] **Principle of uniform boundedness** Banach-Steinhaus theorem and applications.
- [ ] **Inner product spaces and Hilbert spaces** Parallelogram law and orthogonality.
- [ ] **Orthonormal bases and the projection theorem** Bessel's inequality and Parseval's identity.
- [ ] **Riesz representation theorem** Representation of bounded linear functionals on a Hilbert space.
- [ ] **Spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators** Eigenvalue structure and diagonalization.

### Section 8: Numerical Analysis {ma.na}
- [ ] **Direct methods for linear systems** Gaussian elimination, LU decomposition, Cholesky factorization.
  - Pivoting strategies and operation counts
- [ ] **Iterative methods for linear systems** Gauss-Seidel and Jacobi methods and their convergence for diagonally dominant matrices.
- [ ] **Numerical solutions of nonlinear equations** Bisection method, secant method, Newton-Raphson method, fixed point iteration.
  - Order of convergence of each method
- [ ] **Interpolation** Lagrange and Newton forms of the interpolating polynomial.
  - Divided differences
- [ ] **Error in polynomial interpolation** Interpolation error formula.
- [ ] **Numerical differentiation and error** Forward, backward and central difference formulae.
- [ ] **Numerical integration** Trapezoidal and Simpson rules, Newton-Cotes formulae, composite rules and their errors.
  - Degree of precision
- [ ] **Numerical solution of initial value problems** Euler's method and Runge-Kutta method of order 2.

### Section 9: Partial Differential Equations {ma.pde}
- [ ] **Method of characteristics** For first order linear and quasilinear partial differential equations.
- [ ] **Second order PDEs in two independent variables** Classification and canonical forms.
  - Hyperbolic, parabolic and elliptic classification
- [ ] **Method of separation of variables for Laplace equation** In Cartesian and polar coordinates.
- [ ] **Maximum principle** For the Laplace and heat equations.
- [ ] **Wave equation** Cauchy problem, d'Alembert formula, domains of dependence and influence, non-homogeneous wave equation.
- [ ] **Heat equation** Cauchy problem in one space variable.
- [ ] **Laplace and Fourier transform methods** Solving PDEs by integral transforms.

### Section 10: Topology {ma.top}
- [ ] **Basic concepts of topology** Topological spaces, open and closed sets, closure and interior.
- [ ] **Bases and subbases** Generating a topology.
- [ ] **Subspace, order and product topologies** Definitions and standard examples.
- [ ] **Quotient topology and metric topology** Quotient maps and metrizability.
- [ ] **Connectedness and path connectedness** Components and standard counterexamples.
- [ ] **Compactness** Sequentially compact, limit point compact, and their relations.
- [ ] **Tychonoff theorem** Product of compact spaces.
- [ ] **Countability and separation axioms** First and second countable, Hausdorff, regular, normal.
- [ ] **Urysohn's lemma and Tietze extension theorem** Statements and consequences.

### Section 11: Linear Programming {ma.lp}
- [ ] **Linear programming models** Formulation, standard and canonical forms.
- [ ] **Convex sets and extreme points** Geometry of the feasible region.
- [ ] **Basic feasible solution and the graphical method** Correspondence between extreme points and basic feasible solutions.
- [ ] **Simplex method** Pivoting, optimality and the simplex tableau.
- [ ] **Two phase method and revised simplex method** Handling artificial variables.
- [ ] **Infeasible and unbounded models, alternate optima** Detecting each from the tableau.
- [ ] **Duality theory** Weak duality and strong duality; complementary slackness.
- [ ] **Transportation problems** Balanced and unbalanced; initial basic feasible solution by least cost method, north-west corner rule and Vogel's approximation method.
- [ ] **Optimal solution by modified distribution method** MODI method and degeneracy.
- [ ] **Assignment problems and the Hungarian method** Formulation and solution.
