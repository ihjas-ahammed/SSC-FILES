Type 1: $x>a$ (Strictly greater)

All real numbers greater than $a$ ($a$ is not included).

$x>a$
$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x>a\}=(a,\infty)$
$x\ge a$

All real numbers greater than or equal to $a$ ($a$ is included).

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x\ge a\}=[a,\infty)$
$x>-\infty$

All real numbers (no lower restriction).

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x>-\infty\}=\mathbb{R}=(-\infty,\infty)$

Type 2: $x<a$ (Strictly less)

All real numbers less than $a$ ($a$ is not included).

$x<a$
$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x<a\}=(-\infty,a)$
$x\le a$

All real numbers less than or equal to $a$ ($a$ is included).

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x\le a\}=(-\infty,a]$
$x<\infty$

All real numbers (no upper restriction).

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : x<\infty\}=\mathbb{R}=(-\infty,\infty)$

Type 3: $\;a<x<b$ (Between two numbers)

All real numbers strictly between $a$ and $b$ (neither included).

$a<x<b$
$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : a<x<b\}=(a,b)$
$a\le x\le b$

All real numbers between $a$ and $b$ including both endpoints.

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : a\le x\le b\}=[a,b]$
$a\le x<b$

Includes $a$ but not $b$.

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : a\le x<b\}=[a,b)$
$a<x\le b$

Excludes $a$ but includes $b$.

$\{x\in\mathbb{R} : a<x\le b\}=(a,b]$
