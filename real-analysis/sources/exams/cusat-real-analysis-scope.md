# CUSAT CAT — Real Analysis scope

## Source status

CUSAT does not publish a detailed topic-by-topic mathematics syllabus for this
graduate-level entrance test. This scope is the project’s working interpretation of
the CUSAT Mathematics tracker in `Documents/Banana`, aligned to the B.Sc.-level
Real Analysis coverage used by JAM and the university curriculum.

## Real Analysis

### Sequences and Series of Real Numbers

- Convergence of sequences: definitions, limits, and algebra of limits
- Bounded and monotone sequences; monotone convergence theorem
- Cauchy sequences and completeness of the real numbers
- Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem and convergent subsequences
- Absolute and conditional convergence; rearrangements
- Comparison, ratio, and root tests for series
- Power series of one real variable; radius and interval of convergence
- Term-wise differentiation and integration of power series

### Functions of One Real Variable

- Limits and continuity; epsilon–delta definitions
- Intermediate value property and root location
- Differentiation and differentiability versus continuity
- Rolle’s theorem and the mean value theorem
- L’Hospital’s rule
- Taylor’s theorem and Taylor series
- Local and global maxima and minima
- Riemann integration and integrability criteria
- Fundamental theorem of calculus

## Use in this project

Treat this as a CUSAT preparation scope, not an official CUSAT publication. Use
CUSAT-style objective practice and the CUSAT prospectus/test pattern where available;
use the JAM Real Analysis syllabus as the closest detailed public benchmark for topics
outside this focused list.
