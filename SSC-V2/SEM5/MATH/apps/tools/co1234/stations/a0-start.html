MAT5EJ303 (2) Convex Optimization, all four examined modules (70 external marks, at least 15 from each module). Module I is the multivariable calculus of Binmore ch. 19 (printed pp. 190–231). Modules II–IV are Boyd & Vandenberghe ch. 2–5. Work top to bottom and tick as you go.

Where the marks are, and a working order
I
Multivariable calculusFastest marks: directional derivatives, Jacobians, chain rule, the Hessian test. Every exercise of 19.11, 19.26 and 19.47 is worked in the problems section.
II
Convex sets and functionsDefinitions you must state exactly (affine, convex, cone, proper cone, dual cone, convex, quasiconvex, log-concave), and the short proofs: intersections, affine images, first- and second-order conditions.
III
Convex problemsStandard form, the optimality criterion, and turning a problem into an LP, QP, SOCP, GP or SDP.
IV
DualityWrite a Lagrangian, compute the dual function, state weak and strong duality and Slater, write the KKT conditions, Farkas.
