$\sup_{\mathbf z}\inf_{\mathbf w}f\le\inf_{\mathbf w}\sup_{\mathbf z}f$. Proof: $\inf_{\mathbf w}f(\mathbf w,\tilde{\mathbf z})\le f(\tilde{\mathbf w},\tilde{\mathbf z})\le\sup_{\mathbf z}f(\tilde{\mathbf w},\mathbf z)$, then sup over $\tilde{\mathbf z}$, inf over $\tilde{\mathbf w}$. With $f=L$, $\mathbf w=\mathbf x$, $\mathbf z=\boldsymbol\lambda\succeq0$: $\inf_{\mathbf x}\sup_{\boldsymbol\lambda}L=p^\star$ and $\sup_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\inf_{\mathbf x}L=d^\star$, so it is $d^\star\le p^\star$. Equality (saddle point) is strong duality.
