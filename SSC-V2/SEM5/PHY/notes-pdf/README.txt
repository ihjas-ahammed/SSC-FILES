Solid State Physics notes (Properties of Solids), Modules I-IV
Layout of this folder:
  moduleN/single/   one-column A4 PDFs   (module-N-theory.pdf, module-N-derivations.pdf)
  moduleN/two/      two-column A4 PDFs   (same file names)
  moduleN/html/     plain HTML copies    (single-column, open in any browser, no scripts)
  all-modules/{single,two,html}/  all-modules-concept-summary and all-modules-derivation-summary
theory = concepts, results and a "where" list of every symbol; derivations = every derivation step by step
with its figures, the "before you start" list and checkpoints (answers shown).
