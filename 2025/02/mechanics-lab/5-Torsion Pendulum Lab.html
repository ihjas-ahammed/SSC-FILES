Torsion Pendulum Experiment

1. Aim & Apparatus

Aim:

  1. Determine the Rigidity Modulus (G) of the wire material.
  2. Determine Moment of Inertia (I) of the disc using identical masses.
  3. Determine Moment of Inertia (I) by Direct Method.

Apparatus: Torsion pendulum (Disc suspended by wire), Stopwatch, Two identical masses (0.2kg each), Screw gauge, Scale.

2. Principle & Formulas

When the disc is twisted, a restoring couple acts on the wire. The period of oscillation (T) depends on the Moment of Inertia (I) and the couple per unit twist (C).

T=2πIC

A. Rigidity Modulus (G)

G=8πIr4(LT2)mean

B. Moment of Inertia using Masses (Iexp)

By placing two equal masses (m) at distances d1 and d2:

I=2m(d22−d12)T2T22−T12

C. Direct Method (Idirect)

I=MR22

M=Mass of Disc, R=Radius of Disc, r=Radius of Wire, L=Length of Wire.
