Compound Pendulum Experiment

1. Aim & Principle

Aim: To determine acceleration due to gravity (g), Radius of Gyration (k), and Moment of Inertia (ICM) of a bar pendulum, and verify the Parallel Axis Theorem.

Principle: The time period T of a compound pendulum oscillating about an axis at distance l (or d) from the Center of Gravity (C.G.) is:

T=2πk2+l2gl

Where L=k2l+l is the length of the equivalent simple pendulum.

Parallel Axis Theorem Verification:

Ipivot=ICM+Md2
