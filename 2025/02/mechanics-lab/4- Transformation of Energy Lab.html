Transformation of Energy (Rolling Cylinder)

1. Aim & Apparatus

Aim: To demonstrate the conversion of Potential Energy (PE) to Total Kinetic Energy (KEtrans+KErot) for a cylinder rolling down an incline.

Apparatus: Smartphone with Phyphox app, Cylinder (PVC pipe), Sponge for padding, Inclined plane arrangement.

2. Principle

At the top of the incline, the system has only Potential Energy. At the bottom, this energy converts into Translational and Rotational Kinetic Energy.

Initial Energy (Ei) = Final Energy (Ef)

PE=Mgh KETotal=12Mv2+12Iω2

Moment of Inertia (I)

The total moment of inertia consists of the hollow cylinder and the phone (rectangular slab) rotating about the center axis.

I=Icylinder+Iphone I=12McR2+Mp(T2+w2)12

Where: Mc=Mass of Cylinder, Mp=Mass of Phone, R=Radius, T=Phone Thickness, w=Phone Width.
