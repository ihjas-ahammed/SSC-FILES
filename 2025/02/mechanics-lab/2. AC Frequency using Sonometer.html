Frequency of AC using Sonometer

1. Aim & Apparatus

Aim: To find the frequency of alternating current (AC) by using a sonometer and an electromagnet.

Apparatus:

  • Sonometer with soft iron wire stretched over it.
  • Electromagnet with a step-down transformer (220-6V).
  • Slotted weights with hanger.

2. Principle

A wire of length l and mass per unit length m is stretched by a tension force T. When set into transverse vibrations in the fundamental mode, the frequency n is given by:

n=12lTm

Where T=Mg (Mass × Gravity).

Relationship between Wire Frequency (n) and AC Frequency (f):

An AC current changes direction twice per cycle. The electromagnet becomes magnetized during both the positive and negative half-cycles. The soft iron wire is attracted to the magnet regardless of polarity (North or South).

Therefore, the wire is attracted twice in every AC cycle.

Frequency of AC (f) = Frequency of Wire (n)2 f=n2

3. Experimental Setup

The electromagnet is connected to the step-down transformer. It is placed near the center of the wire between the two bridges (knife edges).
