Based on the provided excerpts from "Introduction to Electrodynamics" by David J. Griffiths, I have structured the content into a pedagogical hierarchy.

I have categorized the lessons by identifying specific **Theory** (derivations/concepts), **Definitions** (formal laws/terms), **Examples** (worked solutions), and **Exercise Blocks** (Problems).

---

### **Module I: Vector Analysis (Mathematical Foundations)**

**Section 1.1: Vector Algebra**
*   **111 (Operations & Geometry):** Definition of Vectors vs. Scalars, Theory of Addition/Subtraction, Scalar Multiplication, Dot Product Definition (Eq 1.1), Cross Product Definition (Eq 1.4), Right-hand Rule, Geometric Interpretations (Fig 1.1–1.8). (8 lessons)
*   **112 (Component Algebra):** Theory of Cartesian Coordinates, Unit Vectors ($\hat{x}, \hat{y}, \hat{z}$), Basis Vectors, Component rules for Addition/Dot/Cross products (Eq 1.7–1.14). (6 lessons)
*   **113 (Advanced Products):** Theory of Triple Products (Scalar/Vector), BAC-CAB Rule, Non-associativity of Cross Products, Transformation Laws (Rotations/Tensors). (8 lessons)
*   **114 (Position & Separation):** Definition of Position Vector ($\mathbf{r}$), Infinitesimal Displacement ($d\mathbf{l}$), Definition of Separation Vector ($\boldsymbol{\imath}$), Unit vector $\hat{\boldsymbol{\imath}}$. (5 lessons)
*   **115 (Algebra Practice):** Example 1.1 (Law of Cosines), Example 1.2 (Cube Diagonals), Problems 1.1–1.10. (10 lessons)

**Section 1.2: Differential Calculus**
*   **121 (The Gradient):** Theory of Ordinary vs. Partial Derivatives, Definition of Gradient ($\nabla T$), Geometric Interpretation (Steepest Ascent), Example 1.3. (6 lessons)
*   **122 (The Del Operator):** Definition of the Del Operator ($\nabla$), Theory of $\nabla$ as a vector operator, Overview of Gradient, Divergence, and Curl. (4 lessons)
*   **123 (Divergence):** Definition of Divergence ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}$), Geometric Interpretation (Spread/Flux), Example 1.4, Problems 1.15–1.17. (7 lessons)
*   **124 (The Curl):** Definition of Curl ($\nabla \times \mathbf{v}$), Geometric Interpretation (Swirl/Paddlewheel), Example 1.5, Problems 1.18–1.20. (7 lessons)
*   **125 (Second Derivatives):** Theory of the Laplacian ($\nabla^2$), Product Rules (i-vi), Identity of Curl of Gradient ($=0$), Identity of Divergence of Curl ($=0$), Problems 1.21–1.26. (9 lessons)

**Section 1.3: Integral Calculus & Curvilinear Systems**
*   **131 (Basic Integrals):** Definition of Line Integrals, Surface Integrals (Flux), Volume Integrals, Example 1.6–1.8. (7 lessons)
*   **132 (Fundamental Theorems):** Fundamental Theorem for Gradients, Divergence Theorem (Gauss's), Stokes' Theorem (Curls), Examples 1.9–1.11. (9 lessons)
*   **133 (Spherical & Cylindrical):** Definition of Spherical ($r, \theta, \phi$) and Cylindrical ($s, \phi, z$) Coordinates, Unit vector transformations, Volume elements ($d\tau$), $\nabla$ in curvilinear systems. (10 lessons)
*   **134 (Foundational Exercises):** Problems 1.27–1.65 (Comprehensive Vector Calculus review). (12 lessons)

### **Module II: Electrostatics**

**Section 2.1: The Electric Field**
*   **211 (Forces & Fields):** Introduction to Source vs. Test charges, Theory of Superposition, Definition of Coulomb’s Law (Eq 2.1), Definition of Electric Field (E). (6 lessons)
*   **212 (Continuous Distributions):** Theory of Charge Densities (Line $\lambda$, Surface $\sigma$, Volume $\rho$), Setup of Integrals for E, Example 2.1–2.2. (7 lessons)
*   **213 (Field Exercises):** Problems 2.1–2.8 (Point charges, loops, disks, and shells). (8 lessons)

**Section 2.2: Divergence, Curl, and Potential**
*   **221 (Gauss’s Law):** Theory of Field Lines and Flux, Definition of Gauss’s Law (Integral/Differential), Derivation of Divergence of E. (6 lessons)
*   **222 (Applications of Gauss):** Symmetry types (Spherical, Cylindrical, Planar), Examples 2.3–2.6 (Pillboxes and shells). (8 lessons)
*   **223 (Electric Potential):** Theory of Path Independence, Definition of Potential (V), Poisson’s and Laplace’s Equations, Potential of localized charges, Example 2.7–2.8. (9 lessons)
*   **224 (Boundary & Energy):** Boundary Conditions on E and V, Theory of Work and Energy in Electrostatics, Capacitors (Parallel-plate/Spherical), Example 2.9–2.12. (10 lessons)

**Section 2.3: Conductors**
*   **231 (Basic Properties):** Definition of Ideal Conductors, Theory of $E=0$ inside, Induced Charges, Faraday Cages, Example 2.10. (6 lessons)
*   **232 (Advanced Electrostatics):** Problems 2.31–2.67 (Force on conductors, Diode space-charge, Multipole expansions). (12 lessons)

### **Module III: Magnetostatics**

**Section 5.1: The Lorentz Force Law**
*   **511 (Magnetic Fields):** Introduction to Magnetic Forces, Attraction/Repulsion of Currents, Right-hand rule for B-fields (Fig 5.2). (5 lessons)
*   **512 (Force Theory):** Definition of Lorentz Force Law (Eq 5.1), Theory of Cyclotron/Cycloid Motion, Magnetic forces do no work, Examples 5.1–5.2. (8 lessons)
*   **513 (Current Dynamics):** Definition of Line, Surface, and Volume Currents ($I, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{J}$), The Continuity Equation, Magnetic force on currents, Examples 5.3–5.4. (8 lessons)

**Section 5.2: The Biot-Savart Law**
*   **521 (Magnetic Field Calculation):** Definition of Steady Currents, Definition of Biot-Savart Law (Eq 5.34), Units (Teslas/Gauss). (5 lessons)
*   **522 (Symmetric Configurations):** Magnetic field of a straight wire, Field of a circular loop, Forces between parallel wires, Examples 5.5–5.6. (8 lessons)
*   **523 (Practice):** Problems 5.8–5.13 (Solenoids, polygons, and rotating shells). (7 lessons)

**Section 5.3: Divergence and Curl of B**
*   **531 (Ampère’s Law):** Theory of Straight-Line Currents, Derivation of $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J}$, Definition of Ampère’s Law (Integral form). (6 lessons)
*   **532 (Applications of Ampère):** Symmetry conditions, Infinite planes, Solenoids vs. Toroids, Examples 5.7–5.10. (9 lessons)
*   **533 (Comparison Theory):** Maxwell’s Equations for Statics (E vs B), Theory of Magnetic Monopoles (absence thereof), Magnetic Field Lines "Dirty Secret." (7 lessons)

**Section 5.4: Magnetic Vector Potential**
*   **541 (The Vector Potential):** Definition of Vector Potential ($\mathbf{A}$), Theory of the Coulomb Gauge ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = 0$), Poisson’s Equation for A, Examples 5.11–5.12. (8 lessons)
*   **542 (Multipole Expansion):** Theory of distant potentials, Definition of Magnetic Dipole Moment ($\mathbf{m}$), Coordinate-free form of Dipole field. (6 lessons)
*   **543 (Magnetostatic Practice):** Problems 5.22–5.69 (Hall Effect, Helmholtz coils, Magnetic Monopole math). (12 lessons)
