Melde's String Experiment

1. Aim & Apparatus

Aim: To determine the frequency of an electrically maintained tuning fork.

Apparatus: Electrically maintained tuning fork, pulley, fine thread (string), meter scale, scale pan, weight box, battery/power supply.

2. Experimental Setup

The experiment can be performed in two arrangements (modes):

Transverse Mode

Fork vibrates perpendicular to string.

Longitudinal Mode

Fork vibrates parallel to string.

3. Principle

The frequency of vibration depends on the mode of arrangement.

1. Transverse Mode

The frequency of the string is equal to the frequency of the fork.

n=12lTm=g4m(Ml2)

2. Longitudinal Mode

The frequency of the fork is twice the frequency of the string.

n=2×12lTm=gm(Ml2)

Where: M = Mass in pan (kg), l = Length of one loop (m), m = Mass per unit length of string (kg/m), g = 9.8 m/s².
