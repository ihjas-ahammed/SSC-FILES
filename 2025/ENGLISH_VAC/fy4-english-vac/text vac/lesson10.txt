Unit 10 Don't Be Beautiful Nikita Gill

Introduction Nikita Gill is a British-Indian poet, writer, and artist celebrated
for her emotionally charged poetry that delves into themes of love, healing,
feminism, mental health, and self-empowerment. Born in Belfast, Northern
Ireland, and raised in New Delhi, India, Gill later returned to the United
Kingdom, where she cultivated her literary voice. Her multicultural upbringing,
combined with a profound sensitivity to emotional and social realities, informs
her poetic style. Deeply inspired by literary figures such as Sylvia Plath, Maya
Angelou, and Robert Frost, Gill blends the lyrical with the mythological to
create powerful reflections on the human experience. Her popularity has grown
significantly through social media, where her accessible and intimate poetry
connects with readers across the globe.

Gill has published several acclaimed poetry collections, including Your Soul Is
a River, Wild Embers: Poems of Rebellion, Fire and Beauty, Great Goddesses: Life
Lessons from Myths and Monsters, and Fierce Fairytales: & Other Stories to Stir
Your Soul. These works reimagine ancient myths and classic tales through a
feminist lens, giving voice to resilience, transformation, and female strength.
In addition to her written work, she wrote and performed her debut stage
production Maidens, Myths, and Monsters, which explores themes of mythology and
womanhood. Gill was also the first Instagram poet commissioned by National
Poetry Day in the UK and currently serves as its ambassador. In 2021, she was
named one of The Bookseller's 50 most influential people in British culture. Her
poetry continues to inspire a new generation of readers with its blend of myth,
emotion, and modern relevance.

"Don't Be Beautiful" by Nikita Gill is a powerful feminist poem that challenges
conventional standards of beauty and encourages women to define themselves
beyond appearance. The poem urges readers not to focus on being "beautiful" in
the way society dictates, but instead to be strong, intelligent, passionate, and
brave. Through assertive and empowering language, Gill redefines what it means
to be valued, shifting the focus from physical attributes to inner strength and
individuality.

Text They keep saying that beautiful is something a girl needs to be. But
honestly? Forget that. Don't be beautiful. Be angry, be intelligent, be witty,
be klutzy, be interesting, be funny, be adventurous, be crazy, be talented -
there are an eternity of other things to be other than beautiful. And what is
beautiful anyway but a set of letters strung together to make a word? Be your
own definition of amazing, always. That is so much more important than anything
beautiful, ever.
