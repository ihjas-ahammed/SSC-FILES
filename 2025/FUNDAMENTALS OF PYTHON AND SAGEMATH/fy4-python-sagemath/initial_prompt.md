─── DERIVATION (Der) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Slide 0: Show the starting point AND the final result upfront — student knows the destination
  Slide 1: Interactive Derivation — each step after step 1 is an objective choice ("what comes next?") so the student is constructing the derivation themselves
  Slide 2: Conceptual question about the physical meaning of the result
  Slide 3: Conceptual question about assumptions or limitations used in the derivation
  Slide 4: Numerical application of the derived result

─── EXPERIMENT (Exp) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Slide 0: Historical/physical setup — what was the experiment trying to find? Canvas diagram if useful
  Slide 1: Interactive walkthrough of the experimental logic (what was observed → what it implied)
  Slide 2: Objective question about the experimental observation
  Slide 3: Objective question about the conclusion or implication
  Slide 4: Numerical problem based on the experiment (if applicable)

─── WORKED EXAMPLE — large (Ex, multi-step) ─────────────────────────────────
  Slide 0: The problem itself — ask student to attempt it; include Canvas diagram if needed
  Slide 1: Interactive Solution — step-by-step, guiding student to the answer (not just showing it)
  Slide 2: Objective question about the method used in Step 1 or 2
  Slide 3: Objective question about the physical interpretation of the result
  Slide 4: Numerical variation or follow-up problem

─── EXERCISE / SHORT QUESTION (exe, P — one-step or one-answer) ─────────────
  Slide: The question itself with space to answer or as an objective question
  Note: Group related short questions together on shared slides when appropriate
