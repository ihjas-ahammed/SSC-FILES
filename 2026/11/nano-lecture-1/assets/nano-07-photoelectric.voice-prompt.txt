SCRIPT:
Shine red light on a metal:
nothing.
Shine violet:
electrons fly out.
Why does colour matter?

Light comes in packets:
photons, each h nu.
One photon frees one electron.

The energy budget:
h nu equals phi plus K E.
Phi is the work function, the toll to escape.
Write phi as h nu nought; nu nought is the threshold.

Potassium needs two electron volts.
Red light gives one point seven seven:
too small. No electrons.
Green gives two point two five, and violet three point one:
the extra becomes speed.

Now the three graphs.
Current rises with intensity:
more photons, more electrons.
Current against voltage stops at minus V nought, whatever the intensity.
Energy against frequency:
a line from nu nought, with slope h.

Intensity sets how many.
Frequency sets how fast.
Light acts as a particle.

Quick recap:
photon, budget, threshold, intensity, stopping, slope.

Light comes in packets, each worth h nu.
