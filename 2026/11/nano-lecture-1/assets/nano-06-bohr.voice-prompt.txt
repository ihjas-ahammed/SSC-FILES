SCRIPT:
Why don’t atoms collapse?
Classically, they should.

An electron circles a nucleus of charge Ze.
Coulomb pull supplies the centripetal force.
But a circling charge radiates energy, and spirals inward.

Bohr’s fix: quantize the angular momentum.
m v r comes in steps of h over 2 pi.
Only certain orbits are allowed.
Radius grows as n squared; the first is zero point zero five three nanometers.

Energy goes as Z squared over n squared; hydrogen’s ground state:
minus thirteen point six electron volts.
Jump down a level, and light carries the energy gap: h c over lambda.

Falling to level one:
Lyman, ultraviolet.
To level two:
Balmer, visible.
To level three:
Paschen, infrared.

The same maths describes excitons in quantum dots.
Smaller dot, bluer light.

Quick recap:
collapse, quantize, radius, energy, series, dots.

Fixed orbits, sharp colours.
