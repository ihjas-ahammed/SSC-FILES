Checks on the note:
- Pendulum: the book says a 1 µm pendulum gives "1000 Hz, period 1 ms". Eq. 5.1 actually gives ν = (1/2π)√(9.8/10⁻⁶) ≈ 500 Hz
  (period ≈ 2 ms). The video uses the correct value and the same point (≈ 1000× faster than a 1 m pendulum's ≈ 0.5 Hz).
- The note's "T = 2π√(l/g)" is the period form of the book's Eq. 5.1.
- The (nh/e)√(4πε₀r_n/m) = 2πr_n line is λ = h/(mv) with v from the Coulomb balance mv²/r = e²/(4πε₀r²), i.e. v = e/√(4πε₀ m r).
