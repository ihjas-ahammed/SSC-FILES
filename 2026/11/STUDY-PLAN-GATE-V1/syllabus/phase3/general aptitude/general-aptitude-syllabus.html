GATE DA 2024 — Q.3 1 Mark MCQ
📌 Syllabus Topic: Quantitative Aptitude - Number System & Divisibility
Official Question Statement:
How many 4-digit positive integers divisible by 3 can be formed using only the digits {1, 3,4, 6, 7}, such that no digit appears more than once in a number? (A) 24 (B) 48 (C) 72 (D) 12
📖 Source Textbook: Dinesh Khattar pp. 20–35 (Chapter 1: Number System & Divisibility Rules) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 1 Cutout (p. 1) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
A positive integer is divisible by 3 if and only if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3. Any 4-digit number formed using all four digits {1, 2, 3, 4} without repetition has digit sum 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10. Since 10 is not divisible by 3, no such integer can ever be divisible by 3. Therefore, the number of such positive integers is exactly 0.

GATE DA 2025 — Q.9 2 Marks MCQ
📌 Syllabus Topic: Quantitative Aptitude - Exponents & Quadratic Equations
Official Question Statement:
If a real variable 𝑥 satisfies 3𝑥2 = 27 × 9𝑥, then the value of 2𝑥2 (2𝑥)2 is: (A) 2−1 (B) 20 (C) 23 (D) 215 Data Science & Artificial Intelligence - DA

Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
Express all terms with base 3: 3^(x^2) = 27 * 9^x = 3^3 * (3^2)^x = 3^(2x + 3). Equating exponents yields the quadratic equation x^2 = 2x + 3, or x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0. Factoring gives (x - 3)(x + 1) = 0, so x = 3 or x = -1.
GATE DA 2026 — Q.2 1 Mark MCQ
📌 Syllabus Topic: Quantitative Aptitude - Number Theory & Prime Factorization
Official Question Statement:
The product of the digits of a three-digit number is 70. The sum of the digits of this three-digit number is _____ (A) 12 (B) 14 (C) 16 (D) 18 Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA)
📖 Source Textbook: Dinesh Khattar pp. 20–35 (Chapter 1: Number System & Divisibility) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 1 Cutout (p. 1) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
Prime factorize 70: 70 = 2 * 5 * 7. Since 2, 5, and 7 are all single-digit primes and their product is 70, the only set of three single-digit whole numbers whose product is 70 is {2, 5, 7}. Their sum is 2 + 5 + 7 = 14.
GATE DA 2026 — Q.4 1 Mark MCQ
📌 Syllabus Topic: Quantitative Aptitude - Logarithm Identities
Official Question Statement:
Consider two distinct positive real numbers 𝑚, 𝑛, with 𝑚> 𝑛. Let 𝑥= 𝑛log10(𝑚) and 𝑦= 𝑚log10(𝑛). The relation between 𝑥 and 𝑦 is _______. (A) 𝑥> 𝑦 (B) 𝑥< 𝑦 (C) 𝑥= 𝑦 (D) 𝑥= log10(𝑦)
📖 Source Textbook: Dinesh Khattar pp. 468–475 (Chapter 19: Logarithms & Powers) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 1 Cutout (p. 91) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
By the fundamental power-base exchange identity of logarithms: m^(log_a n) = n^(log_a m). Taking the natural logarithm of both sides proves equivalence: ln(m^(log n)) = log(n) * ln(m) = ln(n^(log m)).

A regular dodecagon (12-sided regular polygon) is inscribed in a circle of radius r cm as shown in the figure. The side of the dodecagon is d cm.
