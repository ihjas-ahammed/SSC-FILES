GATE DA 2024 — Q.44 2 Marks MCQ
📌 Syllabus Topic: Uninformed Search - BFS vs DFS State Expansion Count
Official Question Statement:
Consider a state space where the start state is number 1. The successor function for the state numbered n returns two states numbered n+1 and n+2. Assume that the states in the unexpanded state list are expanded in the ascending order of numbers and the previously expanded states are not added to the unexpanded state list. Which ONE of the following statements about breadth-first search (BFS) and depth-first search (DFS) is true, when reaching the goal state number 6? (A) BFS expands more states than DFS. (B) DFS expands more states than BFS. (C) Both BFS and DFS expand equal number of states. (D) Both BFS and DFS do not reach the goal state number 6.
📖 Source Textbook: AIMA pp. 89–96 (Section 3.4: Uninformed Search Strategies (BFS and DFS)) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 1 Cutout (p. 41) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
BFS expands nodes strictly level-by-level using a FIFO queue: exploring $1 \to 2, 3 \to 4, 5 \to 6$ (goal reached after expanding states 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). In contrast, DFS dives deep along the leftmost successor $n \to n+1$ using a LIFO stack ($1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \to 5 \to 6$), reaching the goal with fewer expansions in this linear chain topology.

Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:

📌 Syllabus Topic: Informed Search - Heuristic Admissibility of Combinations
Official Question Statement:
Let ℎ1and ℎ2 be two admissible heuristics used in 𝐴∗ search. Which ONE of the following expressions is always an admissible heuristic? (A) ℎ1 + ℎ2 (B) ℎ1 × ℎ2 (C) ℎ1/ℎ2, (ℎ2 ≠0) (D) |ℎ1 −ℎ2|
📖 Source Textbook: AIMA pp. 110–122 (Section 3.6: Heuristic Functions (Generating and Combining Heuristics)) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 2 Cutout (p. 14) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
Since $h_1(n)$ and $h_2(n)$ are both admissible heuristics, $0 \le h_1(n) \le h^*(n)$ and $0 \le h_2(n) \le h^*(n)$. Their absolute difference satisfies $0 \le |h_1(n) - h_2(n)| \le \max(h_1(n), h_2(n)) \le h^*(n)$. Because the expression is strictly non-negative and bounded above by $h^*(n)$, $|h_1(n) - h_2(n)|$ is guaranteed to be an admissible heuristic.

S A E B C D G 4 1 2 2 3 3 2 h=2 h=2 h=6 h=2 h=0 h=6 Suppose A∗algorithm is applied on this state graph using priority queue to store the frontier. In what sequence are the nodes expanded? (A) S,A,E,C,B,D,G (B) S,E,A,C,B,D,G (C) S,A,E,B,C,D,G (D) S,A,B,E,C,D,G
📖 Source Textbook: AIMA pp. 98–105 & 110–115 (Section 3.5.2 & 3.6: A* Search & Consistent Heuristics) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 2 Cutout (p. 2) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:

📌 Syllabus Topic: Probabilistic Graphical Models - d-Separation & Conditional Independence
Official Question Statement:
Consider five random variables 𝑈, 𝑉, 𝑊, 𝑋, and 𝑌 whose joint distribution satisfies: 𝑃(𝑈, 𝑉, 𝑊, 𝑋, 𝑌) = 𝑃(𝑈)𝑃(𝑉)𝑃(𝑊|𝑈, 𝑉)𝑃(𝑋|𝑊)𝑃(𝑌|𝑊) Which ONE of the following statements is FALSE? (A) 𝑌 is conditionally independent of 𝑉 given 𝑊 (B) 𝑋 is conditionally independent of 𝑈 given 𝑊 (C) 𝑈 and 𝑉 are conditionally independent given 𝑊 (D) 𝑌 and 𝑋 are conditionally independent given 𝑊 Organizing Institute: IISc Bengaluru
📖 Source Textbook: AIMA pp. 433–440 (Section 13.2: Conditional Independence in Bayesian Networks (d-separation)) • Cutout Link: Open in Module 4 Cutout (p. 88) →
Theoretical Rationale & Exam Strategy:
In the DAG topology U -> W <- V, W is a collider (v-structure). By d-separation, unconditioned colliders block the path (U independent of V). Conditioning on W (or any descendant of W) unblocks/activates the path, inducing conditional dependence between U and V (Berkson's bias). Hence the statement 'U and V are conditionally independent given W' is FALSE.
