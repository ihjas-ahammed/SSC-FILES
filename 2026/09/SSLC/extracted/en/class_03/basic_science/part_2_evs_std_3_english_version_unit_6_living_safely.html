Please don’t do

Don’t rub your eyes if dust falls in to them.

Don’t insert objects into the ear.

If you hit your head and get a swelling, don’t rub it forcefully.

Victims of accidents may have fractures in their bones. Do not lift them up suddenly.

Don’t use toothpaste, honey, etc. on burns

Don’t rub or apply heat on bruises.
