Let’s observe the Moon

The Moon, which is the celestial sphere closest to the Earth, cannot
shine by itself. The Moon reflects the Sun’s light falling on it. This
is the reason for the Moon’s glow and the moonlight at night.
So it is the Sun
itself that gives the
light at night!

How big is the Sun?
Imagine that the Sun is a ball of the size of your classroom. Then
the Earth will be as small as a bead. The moon will be as small as
a mustard seed.
But, if we look at the setting Sun and the full Moon, they will appear

and observe the size from a long distance. Doesn’t the same football
appear large at first and small later?
What could be the reason for this?

The Sun is very far away from the Earth. But how about the Moon?
It is located nearer to the Earth compared to the Sun.

those adjacent to the mountains can be called the high ranges.
The areas in between them are called midlands.
