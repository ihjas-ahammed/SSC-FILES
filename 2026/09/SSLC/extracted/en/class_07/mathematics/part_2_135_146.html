Changing the lengths does not alter the method of finding areas. So we can make a general statement about computing the area of a triangle: The area of any triangle is half the product of one of the sides and the height from this side to the opposite vertex

If we name the side used to compute the area, the base of the triangle and the height to the opposite vertex, the altitude, then this can be shortened a bit: The area of any triangle is half the product of the base and the altitude
