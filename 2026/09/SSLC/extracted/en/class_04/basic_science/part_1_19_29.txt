Environmental Science

Cotyledons

The thick leaf – like part seen in
the plumule of the germinating
pea seed is the cotyledon. The
food required for a seed to
germinate, is stored in the
cotyledons.

The plant uses the food in the cotyledons. So
the cotyledons shrink and decrease in size
as the plant grows.
The plant grows using the food in the
cotyledons till it prepares its own food.
Do you see two cotyledons in a paddy grain
also?
There is only one cotyledon in the paddy
grain.

Plants having two cotyledons are
called dicotyledonous plants
(dicots).

What are the changes that occur when a seed germinates?
Don't you want to know this?
