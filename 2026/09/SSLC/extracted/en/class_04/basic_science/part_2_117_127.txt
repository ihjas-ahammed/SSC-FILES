Food choking
Food getting blocked in the throat is a dangerous situation. The
immediate first aid provided at the time, helps to save life.
Press the stomach with one hand and tap strongly on the back
of the affected person. If the food does not come out even after
this is done for 3-5 times, provide medical assistance
immediately.
