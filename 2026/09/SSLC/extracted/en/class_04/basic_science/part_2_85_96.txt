The Mariner’s Compass

The Mariner’s Compass is an instrument used
to find out directions. There is a magnetic
needle at the centre of this instrument. This
needle always points in the north-south
direction. The specially marked end of the
needle indicates north. We can mark the four
directions keeping the Mariner’s Compass at
the centre of the classroom.
