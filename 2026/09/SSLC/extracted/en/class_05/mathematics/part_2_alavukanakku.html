Did you get the length of any pencil in exact centimetres without having to measure in millimetres? For example, is there an 8 centimetre long pencil? How do we write it in decimal form? Just write 8 centimetres. Since it has no fractional (that is, decimal) part, we can also write it as 8.0 centimetres (though there’s no need to do so now). A pencil cannot be less than one centimetre long; but there are certain things having such lengths (a very small bit of wire, small screws in toys and so on). How do we write the length of such things in decimal form? 6 For example, 6 millimetres means 10 centimetres. It has no whole number part and is just a fraction. So, we can write it as 0.6 centimetres.

Like this, we can write 4 millimetres as 0.4 centimetres and 1 millimetre as 0.1 centimetre.

Different Measures We measure length of pencils or geometry boxes using centimetres and millimetres. But to measure the lengths of a bench or a room, it’s more convenient to use a metre scale or tape. (How do we measure the distance jumped in sports events?) The length of a bench was found to be 2 metres and 13 centimetres upon measuring. How do we write this in terms of metre alone? One metre means 100 centimetres. In other words, one centimetre is 1 1 100 of a metre, that is 100 metre. 13 13 centimetres means 100 metre. 13 So, 2 metres and 13 centimetres means 2 100 metres.

This is written in decimal form as 2.13 metres. So, 4 metres and 78 centimetres can be written in metre in two ways: 78 4 100 metres in fractional form and 4.78 metres in decimal form.

The length of a table was found to be 1metre, 25 centimetres and a little more; when this bit was measured in millimetres, it was found to be four. Hence it is 1 metre, 25 centimetres and 4 millimetres. How do we say this in metre ? First, let’s convert everything less than a metre into millimetres. Instead of 25 centimetres and 4 millimetres, we can say 254 millimetres, isn’t it? 1 metre = 1000 millimetres 1 1 millimetre = 1000 metre 254 So, 254 millimetres is 1000 metre. 254 And we can write 1 metre and 254 millimetres as 1 1000 metres in fractional form.

We write this in decimal form as 1.254 metres. What is the decimal form of 1metre, 20 centimetres and 4 millimetres? First we write it as 1 metre and 204 millimetres. This we can write in 204 fractional form as 1 1000 metres; and then in decimal form as 1.204 metres. This can be done for other measurements also. For example, how do we write 2 litres and 125 millilitres in decimal form? How many millilitres make a litre? 1 litre = 1000 millilitres 1 1 millilitre = 1000 litre 125 So we can write 2 litres and 125 millilitres as 2 1000 litres in fractional form and 2.125 litres in decimal form.

In the same way, what is the relation between kilogram and gram? 1 kilogram = 1000 grams 1 1 gram = 1000 kilogram
