Fraction and Division
If six litres of milk is divided equally among three persons, how much
would each get?
We usually write the answer to this problem as a division:
6÷3= 2
We’ve also seen that if it is 2 litres that is divided like this, then what
each gets is 32 litre. We can write this also as a division:
2 ÷ 3 = 32
Like this, any fraction can be seen as a division also. For example, we
have seen that if a three metre long ribbon is cut into four equal pieces,
each piece would be 34 metre long. Here, we divided three into four. So,
this can also be written as a division:
3 ÷ 4 = 34
