Question 18.

A and B represent two gas cylinders. The gas in cylinder A is completely transferred to cylinder B, keeping the temperature constant.
(a) Compare the gas pressure in cylinder A and Cylinder B. (1)
(b) Which gas law is related to this? (1)
(c) 10 L of gas is kept in a cylinder at 2 atm pressure.
Keeping the temperature constant, the gas is completely transferred to a 20 L cylinder. What is the new pressure of the gas? (2)
Answer:
(a) Pressure in cylinder ‘B’ is greater than that of ‘A’. (Because the volume of B is less than that of A)
(b) Boyle’s law
(c) 1 atm
P1 = 2 atm, V1 = 10 L
P2 = x, V2 = 20 L
P1V1 = P2V2
⇒ 2 × 10 = x × 20
⇒ x = \(\frac{2 \times 10}{20}\) = 1
