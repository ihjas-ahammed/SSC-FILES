Question 12.
Why does Newton’s colour disc appear to be white, when it is rotated at high speed? Explain. (2)
Answer:
Due to the persistence of vision, the disc appears white. All the rays of light from the seven colors reach the retina of the eye within 1/16th of a second.

Question 14.
(a) What is the energy transformation taking place in a moving coil loudspeaker? (1)
(b) Explain the working of a moving coil loudspeaker. (2)
Answer:
(a) Electrical Energy → Sound Energy/Mechanical Energy
(b) The electrical pulses from a microphone are strengthened using an amplifier and sent through the voice coil of a loudspeaker. The voice coil, which is placed in the magnetic field, moves to and fro rapidly, by the electrical pulses. These movements make the diaphragm vibrate, thereby reproducing sound.
