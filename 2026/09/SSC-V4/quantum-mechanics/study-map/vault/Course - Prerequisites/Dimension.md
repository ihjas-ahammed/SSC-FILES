# Dimension

**One-line meaning:** The number of independent directions in a space.

**Formal definition:** The dimension of a finite-dimensional [[Vector Space|vector space]] is the [[Real Number|number]] of [[Vector|vectors]] in any [[Basis|basis]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 6 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\mathbb C^2$ has dimension $2$.
