# Symmetric Part

**One-line meaning:** The half-sum of two opposite-order products.

**Formal definition:** The symmetric part is $\tfrac12(AB+BA)$ and is unchanged under exchanging $A$ and $B$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $A=B$ the symmetric part is $A^2$.
