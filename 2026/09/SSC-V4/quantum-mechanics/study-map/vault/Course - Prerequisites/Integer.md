# Integer

**One-line meaning:** A whole number, including zero and negative whole numbers.

**Formal definition:** Integers are $\ldots,-2,-1,0,1,2,\ldots$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 31 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $-3$ is an integer; $1/2$ is not.
