# Expectation Brackets

**One-line meaning:** Angle brackets around an observable mean its mean result in the chosen state.

**Formal definition:** For a [[Normalization|normalized]] [[Ket]], $\langle A\rangle=\langle\psi|A|\psi\rangle$ is the [[Expectation Value]]. This shorthand hides the chosen state, which must be specified.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 6 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Equal probabilities of 1 and 3 give $\langle A\rangle=2$.
