# Probability Amplitude

**One-line meaning:** A complex number whose squared magnitude gives a probability or density.

**Formal definition:** A [[Quantum State|state]] coefficient is a probability amplitude; its squared [[Absolute Value|absolute value]] gives a discrete [[Probability|probability]], or a density for continuously labelled states.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 18 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Amplitude $i/\sqrt2$ has squared magnitude $1/2$.
