# Antisymmetric Part

**One-line meaning:** The half-difference between two opposite-order products.

**Formal definition:** $AB=\tfrac12(AB+BA)+\tfrac12(AB-BA)$. The second term is the antisymmetric part under swapping $A,B$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** It changes sign when A and B are exchanged.
