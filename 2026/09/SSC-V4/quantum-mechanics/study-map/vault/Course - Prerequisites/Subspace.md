# Subspace

**One-line meaning:** A smaller collection inside a vector space that still follows the same addition and scaling rules.

**Formal definition:** A subspace $W$ of a [[Vector Space]] $V$ contains the [[Zero Vector]] and is closed under [[Linear Combination|linear combinations]]: $u,v\in W$ implies $au+bv\in W$ for [[Scalar|scalars]] $a,b$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The vectors $(x,0)$ form a subspace of the two-dimensional plane.
