# Trace Notation

**One-line meaning:** tr asks you to add the diagonal entries.

**Formal definition:** $\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_iA_{ii}$ is the [[Trace]] of a finite [[Matrix]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{diag}(2,3)=5$.
