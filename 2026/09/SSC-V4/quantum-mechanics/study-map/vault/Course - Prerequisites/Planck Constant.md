# Planck Constant

**One-line meaning:** The constant setting the size of quantum effects.

**Formal definition:** $\hbar=h/(2\pi)$ is the reduced Planck constant. It has units of action, energy multiplied by time.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 7 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $[x,p]=i\hbar I$.
