# Commutator

**One-line meaning:** The difference between applying two operators in opposite orders.

**Formal definition:** $[A,B]=AB-BA$. Commuting operators satisfy $[A,B]=0$. The product rule is $[A,BC]=[A,B]C+B[A,C]$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 5 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $[x,p]=i\hbar I$ in the canonical position–momentum setting.
