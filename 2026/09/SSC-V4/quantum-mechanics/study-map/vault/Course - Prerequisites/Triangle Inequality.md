# Triangle Inequality

**One-line meaning:** The length of a sum does not exceed the sum of the lengths.

**Formal definition:** $\|u+v\|\le\|u\|+\|v\|$. The [[Norm|norm]] induced by an [[Inner Product|inner product]] satisfies this inequality.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $u=(1,0)$, $v=(0,1)$, $\sqrt2\le2$.
