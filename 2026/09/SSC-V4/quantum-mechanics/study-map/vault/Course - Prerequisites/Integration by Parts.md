# Integration by Parts

**One-line meaning:** Move a derivative from one factor to the other inside an integral.

**Formal definition:** $\int_a^b u(x)v\prime(x)dx=[uv]_a^b-\int_a^b u\prime(x)v(x)dx$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Boundary terms vanish for suitably decaying functions on the whole line.
