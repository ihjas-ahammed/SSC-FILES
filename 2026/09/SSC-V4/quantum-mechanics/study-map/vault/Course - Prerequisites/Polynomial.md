# Polynomial

**One-line meaning:** A finite sum of nonnegative integer powers with numerical coefficients.

**Formal definition:** $f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^n c_kx^k$, where $n$ is a nonnegative [[Integer|integer]] and each $c_k$ is a [[Scalar|scalar]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 4 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $x^2-5x+5$ is a polynomial.
