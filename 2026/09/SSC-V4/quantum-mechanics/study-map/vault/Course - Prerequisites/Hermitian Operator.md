# Hermitian Operator

**One-line meaning:** An operator equal to its adjoint in the setting under consideration.

**Formal definition:** In finite dimensions, Hermitian means $A=A^\dagger$, equivalently $\langle u|Av\rangle=\langle Au|v\rangle$. For [[Operator Domain|unbounded]] operators, distinguish this [[Symmetric Operator|symmetry identity]] from [[Self-adjoint Operator|self-adjointness]], which also requires equality of domains.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 10 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\begin{pmatrix}2&-i\\i&3\end{pmatrix}$ is Hermitian.
