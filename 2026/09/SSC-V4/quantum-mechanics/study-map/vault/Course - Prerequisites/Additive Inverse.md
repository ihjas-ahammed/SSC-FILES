# Additive Inverse

**One-line meaning:** A vector that cancels another vector when added.

**Formal definition:** For every [[Vector|vector]] $v$, its additive inverse $-v$ satisfies $v+(-v)=0$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The inverse of $(2,-1)$ is $(-2,1)$.
