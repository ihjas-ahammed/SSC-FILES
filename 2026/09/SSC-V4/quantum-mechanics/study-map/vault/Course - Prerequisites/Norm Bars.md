# Norm Bars

**One-line meaning:** Double bars measure the length of a vector.

**Formal definition:** $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v|v\rangle}$ is the [[Norm]] induced by an [[Inner Product]]. It differs from a [[Ket]] delimiter $|v\rangle$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\|(3,4)\|=5$.
