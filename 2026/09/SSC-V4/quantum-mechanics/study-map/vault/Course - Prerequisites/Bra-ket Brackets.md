# Bra-ket Brackets

**One-line meaning:** Angle brackets and vertical bars distinguish states from their pairings.

**Formal definition:** $|\psi\rangle$ is a [[Ket]], $\langle\phi|$ is a [[Bra]], and $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ is their [[Inner Product]]. The order matters for [[Complex Conjugate|complex conjugation]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 23 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For two orthogonal unit states, $\langle 1|2\rangle=0$.
