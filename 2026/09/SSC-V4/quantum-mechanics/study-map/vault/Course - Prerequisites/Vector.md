# Vector

**One-line meaning:** An object that can be added to another of its kind and multiplied by a number.

**Formal definition:** For a [[Coordinate|coordinate]] example, $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ has [[Scalar|scalar]] entries; [[Basic Algebra|addition]] and scaling act entry by entry. Abstract vectors may instead be functions or [[Quantum State|state]] symbols with specified [[Basic Algebra|addition]] and scaling.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(1,2)+(3,4)=(4,6)$.
