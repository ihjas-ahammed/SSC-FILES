# Exponential Notation

**One-line meaning:** A raised expression on e is the input to the exponential function.

**Formal definition:** $e^z=\exp(z)$ is an [[Exponential Function]], not the product $ez$. Its [[Complex Number Arithmetic|complex]] input appears in Fourier kernels.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 8 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $e^0=1$.
