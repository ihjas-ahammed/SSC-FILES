# Cauchy Sequence

**One-line meaning:** A sequence whose later terms get arbitrarily close to each other.

**Formal definition:** For every $\epsilon>0$, there is $N$ with $\|v_n-v_m\|<\epsilon$ whenever $n,m>N$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The partial sums of $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}2^{-k}$ are Cauchy.
