# Invariant Subspace

**One-line meaning:** A smaller vector space that an operation never sends you outside.

**Formal definition:** A [[Subspace]] $W$ is invariant under a [[Linear Operator]] $A$ when $Aw\in W$ for every $w\in W$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$, the line $(x,0)$ is invariant.
