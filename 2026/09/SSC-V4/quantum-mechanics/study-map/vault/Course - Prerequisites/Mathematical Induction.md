# Mathematical Induction

**One-line meaning:** A proof that starts with one case and shows how to pass to the next.

**Formal definition:** Prove a statement for $n=1$, then prove that its truth for $n$ implies its truth for $n+1$. It then holds for all positive integers.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The finite spectral-theorem proof reduces dimension until the one-dimensional case.
