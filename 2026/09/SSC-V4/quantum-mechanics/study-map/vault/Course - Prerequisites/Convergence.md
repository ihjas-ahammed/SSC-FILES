# Convergence

**One-line meaning:** The terms of a sequence approach a vector in distance.

**Formal definition:** A [[Sequence|sequence]] $v_n$ converges in [[Norm|norm]] to $v$ if $\|v_n-v\|\to0$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 30 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(1/n,0)$ converges to $(0,0)$.
