# Boundary Condition

**One-line meaning:** A restriction on a function at the edge of its allowed region.

**Formal definition:** Boundary conditions specify endpoint behavior, such as vanishing or periodic values. They help select the [[Operator Domain|operator domain]] and justify removal of boundary terms.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Smooth decaying wave functions have zero boundary products at infinity.
