# Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

**One-line meaning:** Position and momentum spreads cannot both be arbitrarily small.

**Formal definition:** Using $[x,p]=i\hbar I$ gives $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$ for [[Normalization|normalized]] states with finite variances and suitable domains.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Halving a minimum-width position spread requires at least doubling the corresponding minimum momentum spread.
