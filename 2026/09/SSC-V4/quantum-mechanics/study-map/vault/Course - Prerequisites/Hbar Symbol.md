# Hbar Symbol

**One-line meaning:** ℏ is the reduced Planck constant used in quantum formulas.

**Formal definition:** $\hbar=h/(2\pi)$, where $h$ is the [[Planck Constant]]. It fixes the scale in the [[Commutator]] $[\hat x,\hat p]=i\hbar I$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 6 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The [[Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle]] has lower bound $\hbar/2$.
