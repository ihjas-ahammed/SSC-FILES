# Identity Operator

**One-line meaning:** The operator that leaves every vector unchanged.

**Formal definition:** $I|v\rangle=|v\rangle$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 19 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Its two-dimensional matrix has diagonal entries 1 and off-diagonal entries 0.
