# Orthonormal Basis

**One-line meaning:** A basis of unit-length vectors that have zero overlap with each other.

**Formal definition:** $\langle e_i|e_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}$, where the [[Real Number|number]] $\delta_{ij}$ is 1 if $i=j$ and 0 otherwise. These [[Vector|vectors]] form a [[Basis|basis]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 17 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $(1,0),(0,1)$ form an orthonormal basis.
