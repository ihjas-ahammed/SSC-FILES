# Multiplicity

**One-line meaning:** The number of times a value appears as a repeated polynomial root.

**Formal definition:** Algebraic multiplicity of an [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalue]] is its multiplicity as a [[Polynomial Root|root]] of $\det(A-\lambda I)$. For [[Hermitian Operator|Hermitian]] matrices it equals the [[Dimension|dimension]] of the [[Eigenspace|eigenspace]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $2I$ in two dimensions, $(2-\lambda)^2$ gives multiplicity 2.
