# Ket

**One-line meaning:** A notation for a quantum state vector.

**Formal definition:** $|\psi\rangle$ denotes a [[Vector|vector]] in the [[Hilbert Space|Hilbert space]]. In a chosen [[Basis|basis]] it can be represented as a column of coefficients.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $|\psi\rangle=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}/\sqrt2$.
