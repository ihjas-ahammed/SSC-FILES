# Mathematical Space

**One-line meaning:** A collection of objects equipped with specified rules or relationships.

**Formal definition:** A mathematical space consists of a [[Set|set]] together with additional structure, such as rules for adding its elements or measuring distances between them. Its definition specifies exactly which rules are required.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** A vector space adds addition and scaling rules to a set of vectors.
