# Transpose

**One-line meaning:** Swap the rows and columns of a matrix.

**Formal definition:** $(A^T)_{ij}=A_{ji}$. Transposition alone does not complex-conjugate entries.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 13 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The transpose of $\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}$ is $(1,i)$.
