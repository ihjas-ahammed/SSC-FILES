# Positive Definiteness

**One-line meaning:** A rule assigns a positive self-value to every nonzero vector.

**Formal definition:** A pairing is positive definite when its self-value is real and positive for each nonzero [[Vector|vector]], and is zero for the [[Zero Vector|zero vector]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 30 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The sum $|v_1|^2+|v_2|^2$ is positive unless both entries vanish.
