# Variance

**One-line meaning:** The mean squared distance of measurement results from their average.

**Formal definition:** $(\Delta A)^2=\langle(A-\langle A\rangle I)^2\rangle=\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Equal outcomes 2 and 4 have mean 3 and variance 1.
