# Covariance

**One-line meaning:** The symmetric average of two centered observables multiplied together.

**Formal definition:** With $A\prime=A-\langle A\rangle I$ and $B\prime=B-\langle B\rangle I$, $\operatorname{Cov}(A,B)=\tfrac12\langle\{A\prime,B\prime\}\rangle$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** If $A=B$, covariance equals the variance of $A$.
