# Eigenspace

**One-line meaning:** All vectors having a chosen eigenvalue, with the zero vector included.

**Formal definition:** The eigenspace for $\lambda$ is $E_\lambda=\{v:Av=\lambda v\}$. It is a [[Vector Space|vector space]]; its nonzero members are [[Eigenvector|eigenvectors]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 4 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** For $A=2I$, $E_2$ is the entire space.
