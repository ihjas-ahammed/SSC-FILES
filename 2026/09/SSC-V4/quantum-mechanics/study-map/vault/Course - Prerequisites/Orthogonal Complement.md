# Orthogonal Complement

**One-line meaning:** All vectors perpendicular to every vector in a chosen subspace.

**Formal definition:** $W^\perp=\{v:\langle w|v\rangle=0\text{ for every }w\in W\}$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** The orthogonal complement of the horizontal line in $\mathbb R^2$ is the vertical line.
