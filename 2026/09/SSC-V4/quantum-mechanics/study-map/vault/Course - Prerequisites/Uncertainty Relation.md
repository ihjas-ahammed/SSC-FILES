# Uncertainty Relation

**One-line meaning:** A lower bound on the product of two measurement spreads.

**Formal definition:** For suitable states and [[Hermitian Operator|Hermitian]] operators with the required products defined, $\Delta A\Delta B\ge\tfrac12|\langle[A,B]\rangle|$. This is the Robertson bound.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Noncommuting operators can constrain how small both spreads are.
