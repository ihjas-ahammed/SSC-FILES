# Anticommutator Braces

**One-line meaning:** Braces containing two operations mean AB + BA.

**Formal definition:** $\{A,B\}=AB+BA$ denotes the [[Anticommutator]]. The braces here do not denote a [[Set]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 1 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\{I,A\}=2A$.
