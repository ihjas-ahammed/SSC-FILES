# Probability Density

**One-line meaning:** A nonnegative function whose integral over an interval gives probability.

**Formal definition:** $\rho(x)\ge0$, $\int\rho(x)dx=1$, and the [[Probability|probability]] in $[a,b]$ is $\int_a^b\rho(x)dx$. Density at a point is not itself an interval [[Probability|probability]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 18 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** If $\rho(x)=1$ on $[0,1]$, the probability in $[0,1/4]$ is $1/4$.
