# Commutator Brackets

**One-line meaning:** Square brackets containing two operations mean AB − BA.

**Formal definition:** $[A,B]=AB-BA$ denotes the [[Commutator]]. It checks whether the order of an [[Operator Product]] matters.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 4 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** If $AB=BA$, then $[A,B]=0$.
