# Standard Deviation

**One-line meaning:** The nonnegative square root of the variance.

**Formal definition:** $\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2}$. It is the uncertainty used in the quantum [[Uncertainty Relation|uncertainty relation]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Variance 4 means standard deviation 2.
