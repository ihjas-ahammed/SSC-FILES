# Vector Space

**One-line meaning:** A collection of vectors that obey consistent rules for addition and scaling.

**Formal definition:** A vector space $V$ over a [[Field|field]] $F$ is closed under [[Basic Algebra|addition]] and [[Scalar|scalar multiplication]]. [[Basic Algebra|Addition]] is [[Associativity|associative]] and [[Commutativity|commutative]], has a [[Zero Vector|zero vector]] and additive inverses; $a(u+v)=au+av$, $(a+b)v=av+bv$, $(ab)v=a(bv)$, and $1v=v$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $\mathbb C^2$ with entrywise operations is a vector space. The set of positive real numbers is not: it lacks zero and negatives.
