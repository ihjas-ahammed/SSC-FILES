# Closure

**One-line meaning:** Combining allowed objects produces another allowed object.

**Formal definition:** A [[Set|set]] $V$ is closed under [[Basic Algebra|addition]] and [[Scalar|scalar multiplication]] when $u+v\in V$ and $av\in V$ for all $u,v\in V$ and allowed [[Scalar|scalars]] $a$.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 32 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Real coordinate pairs stay real coordinate pairs after addition.
