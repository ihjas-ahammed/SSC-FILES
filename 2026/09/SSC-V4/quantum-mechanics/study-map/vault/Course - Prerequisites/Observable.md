# Observable

**One-line meaning:** A measurable physical quantity represented by an appropriate operator.

**Formal definition:** Quantum observables are represented by [[Self-adjoint Operator|self-adjoint]] operators, with measurement outcomes described by their [[Spectrum|spectra]]. In finite dimensions these are [[Hermitian Operator|Hermitian]] matrices and their [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]].

**Why it matters here:** Supports 6 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** Position and momentum are observables.
