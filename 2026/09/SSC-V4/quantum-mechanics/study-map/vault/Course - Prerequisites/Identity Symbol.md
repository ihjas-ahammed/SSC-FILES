# Identity Symbol

**One-line meaning:** I means the operation that leaves its input unchanged.

**Formal definition:** $I$ is the [[Identity Operator]]; $I|\psi\rangle=|\psi\rangle$. Its [[Matrix Representation]] has ones on the main diagonal and zeros elsewhere.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 7 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $I_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$.
