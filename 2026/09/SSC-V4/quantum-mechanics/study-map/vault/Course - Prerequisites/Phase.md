# Phase

**One-line meaning:** The angle describing the direction of a complex number.

**Formal definition:** A nonzero [[Complex Number Arithmetic|complex number]] is $z=|z|e^{i\theta}$; $\theta$ is its phase. Multiplying an entire [[Quantum State|state]] by $e^{i\theta}$ gives a global phase without changing [[Probability|probabilities]]; relative phases between coefficients can change interference.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 29 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $i$ has phase $\pi/2$ relative to positive real numbers.
