# Fundamental Theorem of Algebra

**One-line meaning:** Every nonconstant polynomial with complex coefficients has a complex root.

**Formal definition:** If $p(z)$ is a nonconstant complex [[Polynomial|polynomial]], there exists a [[Complex Number Arithmetic|complex number]] $\lambda$ with $p(\lambda)=0$. A degree-n [[Polynomial|polynomial]] has n [[Polynomial Root|roots]] counted with their repeated occurrences.

**Why it matters here:** Supports 2 Module 3 questions.

**Tiny example:** $z^2+1$ has roots $i$ and $-i$.
