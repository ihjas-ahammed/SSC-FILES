# Question
What is the momentum-space wave function of a state |ψ ⟩ ?

# Formal Answer
The [[Momentum Representation|momentum-space wave function]] is $\phi(p)=\langle p|\psi\rangle$.
For a [[Normalization|normalized]] [[Quantum State|state]], $|\phi(p)|^2dp$ is the [[Probability|probability]] of finding momentum in a small interval of width $dp$.
