# Question
Given a state vector |ψ⟩, its position-space wavefunction is ψ(x) = ⟨x|ψ⟩ and its momentum-space wavefunction is ϕ(p) = ⟨p|ψ⟩. Using the continuous completeness relation, show that ψ(x) and ϕ(p) form a Fourier transform pair.

# Formal Answer
1. insert the position [[Completeness Relation|completeness relation]]: $\phi(p)=\langle p|I|\psi\rangle=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\langle p|x\rangle\langle x|\psi\rangle dx$.

2.
Use $\langle p|x\rangle=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}e^{-ipx/\hbar}$ to obtain $\phi(p)=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-ipx/\hbar}\psi(x)dx$.

3.
Conversely, insert $I=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|p\rangle\langle p|dp$ into $\psi(x)=\langle x|I|\psi\rangle$ to get $\psi(x)=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{ipx/\hbar}\phi(p)dp$.
These are the forward and inverse [[Fourier Transform|Fourier transforms]].
