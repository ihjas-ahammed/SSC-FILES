# Question
Define the scalar product of two state vectors | ψ ⟩ and | ϕ ⟩ .

# Formal Answer
The [[Inner Product|scalar product]] $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ is the [[Complex Number Arithmetic|complex number]] obtained by pairing the [[Bra|bra]] of $|\phi\rangle$ with $|\psi\rangle$.

In an [[Orthonormal Basis|orthonormal basis]], $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle=\sum_n\phi_n^*\psi_n$; it is linear in the second slot, conjugate-symmetric, and positive on a nonzero [[Vector|vector]] paired with itself.
