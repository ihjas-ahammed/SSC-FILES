# Question
What is a degenerate eigenvalue?

# Formal Answer
An [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalue]] is [[Degeneracy|degenerate]] when two or more linearly independent [[Eigenvector|eigenvectors]] have that same [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalue]].
Its [[Degeneracy|degeneracy]] is the [[Dimension|dimension]] of the [[Eigenspace|eigenspace]].
