# Question
Define a linear vector space and list any three properties that its vectors must satisfy.

# Formal Answer
A [[Vector Space|vector space]] $V$ over a [[Field|field]] $F$ is a [[Set|set]] equipped with [[Vector|vector]] addition and [[Scalar|scalar]] multiplication, satisfying the [[Vector|vector]]-[[Mathematical Space|space]] [[Axiom|axioms]].

[[Closure|Closure]] means $u+v\in V$ and $au\in V$ for $u,v\in V$ and $a\in F$.

Two of the requested three properties are [[Commutativity|commutativity]], $u+v=v+u$, and [[Associativity|associativity]], $(u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$.

The third is the additive identity: a [[Zero Vector|zero vector]] $0\in V$ exists such that $v+0=v$ for every $v\in V$.
