# Question
Explain how the position-space and momentum-space wave functions are related by a Fourier transform. Write the integral transformation from ψ(x) to ϕ(p). Explain the physical meaning of |ψ(x)|² and |ϕ(p)|².

# Formal Answer
1. Choose $\langle x|p\rangle=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}e^{ipx/\hbar}$. Inserting the position [[Completeness Relation|completeness relation]] into $\phi(p)=\langle p|\psi\rangle$ gives $\phi(p)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\langle p|x\rangle\langle x|\psi\rangle\,dx$.

2. [[Conjugate Symmetry|Conjugate symmetry]] gives $\langle p|x\rangle=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}e^{-ipx/\hbar}$, hence $\phi(p)=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-ipx/\hbar}\psi(x)\,dx$. Conversely $\psi(x)=(2\pi\hbar)^{-1/2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{ipx/\hbar}\phi(p)\,dp$.

3. $|\psi(x)|^2$ and $|\phi(p)|^2$ are position and momentum [[Probability|probability]] densities. For an interval $[a,b]$, the position [[Probability|probability]] is $\int_a^b|\psi(x)|^2\,dx$; momentum [[Probability|probabilities]] are analogous. For a [[Normalization|normalized]] [[Quantum State|state]] the full [[Integral|integrals]] of both densities equal 1.
