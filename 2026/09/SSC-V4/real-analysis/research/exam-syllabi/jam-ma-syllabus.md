# IIT JAM MA — Mathematics

*Source: official JAM 2027 Mathematics (MA) syllabus (IIT Kharagpur). Pattern: 60 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours. Section A — 30 MCQs (10 x 1 mark, 20 x 2 marks) with negative marking; Section B — 10 MSQs (2 marks each, no negative marking, no partial credit); Section C — 20 NAT (10 x 1 mark, 10 x 2 marks, no negative marking).*

## Part 1: Mathematics - 100 marks

*Three official sections, expanded below into the sub-blocks named in the syllabus. All are compulsory.*

### Section 1: Real Analysis

#### Sequences and Series of Real Numbers {ma.seq}
- [ ] **Convergence of sequences** Definition, limits and algebra of limits.
- [ ] **Bounded and monotone sequences** Monotone convergence theorem.
- [ ] **Cauchy sequences** Cauchy criterion and completeness of R.
- [ ] **Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem** Existence of convergent subsequences.
- [ ] **Absolute convergence** Absolute vs. conditional convergence; rearrangement.
- [ ] **Tests of convergence for series** Comparison test, ratio test, root test.
  - When the ratio and root tests are inconclusive
- [ ] **Power series of one real variable** Radius and interval of convergence.
- [ ] **Term-wise differentiation and integration of power series** Validity inside the interval of convergence.

#### Functions of One Real Variable {ma.func}
- [ ] **Limit and continuity** Epsilon-delta definition and algebra of continuous functions.
- [ ] **Intermediate value property** Statement and applications to root location.
- [ ] **Differentiation** Rules of differentiation and differentiability vs. continuity.
- [ ] **Rolle's theorem and the mean value theorem** Statements, hypotheses and applications.
- [ ] **L'Hospital rule** Indeterminate forms and correct use.
- [ ] **Taylor's theorem and Taylor's series** Remainder terms and expansions.
- [ ] **Maxima and minima** First and second derivative tests, global extrema.
- [ ] **Riemann integration** Definite integrals and their properties, integrability criteria.
- [ ] **Fundamental theorem of calculus** Both forms and their use.

### Section 2: Multivariable Calculus and Differential Equations

#### Functions of Two or Three Real Variables {ma.mvc}
- [ ] **Limit and continuity in several variables** Path dependence of limits.
  - Showing a limit does not exist
- [ ] **Partial derivatives** Computation and equality of mixed partials.
- [ ] **Total derivative** Differentiability and the chain rule.
- [ ] **Maxima and minima of several variables** Critical points, second derivative test, saddle points.

#### Integral Calculus {ma.int}
- [ ] **Double integrals** Evaluation over general regions.
- [ ] **Change of order of integration** Sketching regions and rewriting limits.
- [ ] **Triple integrals** Evaluation in Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical coordinates.
- [ ] **Surface areas and volumes using double integrals** Standard applications.
- [ ] **Volumes using triple integrals** Setting up the correct limits.

#### Differential Equations {ma.de}
- [ ] **Bernoulli's equation** Reduction to linear form.
- [ ] **Exact differential equations and integrating factors** Exactness test and standard integrating factors.
- [ ] **Orthogonal trajectories** Families of curves and their orthogonal families.
- [ ] **Homogeneous differential equations** Substitution y = vx and separation.
- [ ] **Method of separation of variables** First order separable equations.
- [ ] **Linear differential equations of second order with constant coefficients** Auxiliary equation and particular integrals.
- [ ] **Method of variation of parameters** Wronskian and particular solutions.
- [ ] **Cauchy-Euler equation** Equidimensional equations and their solutions.

### Section 3: Linear Algebra and Algebra

#### Basic Algebra {ma.basic}
- [ ] **Permutations and combinations** Counting principles and standard arrangements.
- [ ] **Binomial theorem** General term, middle term and coefficient problems.

#### Matrices {ma.mat}
- [ ] **Systems of linear equations** Consistency and the nature of solutions.
- [ ] **Rank, nullity and the rank-nullity theorem** Computation by row reduction.
- [ ] **Inverse and determinant** Properties, adjoint and Cramer's rule.
- [ ] **Eigenvalues and eigenvectors** Characteristic polynomial, algebraic and geometric multiplicity.
  - Cayley-Hamilton theorem and its uses

#### Finite Dimensional Vector Spaces {ma.vs}
- [ ] **Linear independence of vectors** Testing independence and spanning sets.
- [ ] **Basis and dimension** Extending and reducing to a basis.
- [ ] **Linear transformations** Definition, kernel and image.
- [ ] **Matrix representation of linear transformations** With respect to given bases.
- [ ] **Range space and null space** Rank-nullity theorem for linear maps.

#### Groups {ma.grp}
- [ ] **Cyclic groups** Generators, order of elements and subgroups of cyclic groups.
- [ ] **Abelian and non-abelian groups** Standard examples and counterexamples.
- [ ] **Permutation groups** Cycle notation, parity and the symmetric group.
- [ ] **Normal subgroups and quotient groups** Criteria for normality and construction of quotients.
- [ ] **Lagrange's theorem for finite groups** Order of subgroups and corollaries.
- [ ] **Group homomorphisms** Kernel, image and the first isomorphism theorem.
