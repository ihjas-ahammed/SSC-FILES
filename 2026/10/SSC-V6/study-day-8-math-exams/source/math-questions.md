# Maths questions and self-marking

Preserve both complete source papers in their original A/B/C order.
Retain numerical data, all requested proofs and the full Algebra Q19 group table.
Recover TeX from the matching source HTML where PDF extraction loses symbols.

Author marking rubrics from the worked proofs. Allocate realistic full,
partial and zero credit; maxima sum exactly to the original 3/6/10 marks.
Rubrics are practice guidance and do not claim to be official exam schemes.
Never semantically grade a typed proof.

Low-mark flips are the original Section A questions only, with an authored
recallAnswer covering every subpart. Eight words allows reference comparison;
confirmation and skips do not award exam marks. Full calculations/proofs can
be written on paper and assessed using the rubric.

Higher-mark questions remain paper tasks, followed by rubric, score and full
model proof. Maths does not inherit the physics-only requirement of three
word-completion OMR cards per Remember key. Do not duplicate the same recall
question. Animated equality derivations have four authored decision checkpoints.

All mathematical option text supports MathJax. Use explicit TeX for formulas,
sets, fractions and subscripts; keep prose outside the delimiters.
