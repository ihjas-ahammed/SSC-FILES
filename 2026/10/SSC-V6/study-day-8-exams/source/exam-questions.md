# Original questions, self-marking and recall

Read the PDFs and TeX-preserving source HTML. Preserve all questions, numerical
data, subparts and requested figures in order. Write rubrics directly from the
solutions with realistic full/partial/zero options and teaching explanations.
Their maxima sum to 3, 6 or 10. They are practice rubrics, not official marking.

Only the original 3-mark questions flip. Their recallAnswer must address every
subpart. Eight words is a readiness gate, not a grade. Students compare the
reference, then self-mark complete work with eval cards. Drawings and calculations
may be on paper. Confirming/skipping recall awards no exam marks or football shots.

After each model answer and Remember key, author three distinct word-completion
OMR cards, each with one ____, four word choices, practice "remember-word", time
45, correct zero-based answer and why. No formula/number choices or repeated
rubric prompts. The app shuffles the choices.

OMR cards reveal on arrival. Their real-second countdown pauses off-card, in
Match centre, during pause/breaks and while the marks dialog is open.
Their results affect match goals/coverage, not exam marks.
Derivations each have four directly authored decision checkpoints.
