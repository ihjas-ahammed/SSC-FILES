# Direct definitions and linked terms

Answer what something is before listing its uses: a category and plain meaning,
then relevant properties, example and formal expression. Impedance is a complex
voltage/current ratio. A zone plate is an alternating diffractive ring element.

Author glossary entries directly: id, term, definition; aliases/example optional.
Aliases must name the same concept. Distinguish H from B, amplitude from
intensity, peak from rms, angular frequency from hertz, and first-zero half-width
from half-power width.

Open prerequisite definitions only on click/tap or keyboard activation. No hover
or focus activation. Escape/close dismisses; Previous term navigates back.
Do not link inside TeX, SVGs, inputs or choice buttons. Use defines metadata
to suppress a card's own defined terms and aliases.

In these exam papers, info cards do not flip even if they define a term.
The explicit low-mark question scope overrides general definition-card selection:
only 3-mark original questions with an authored recallAnswer flip.
