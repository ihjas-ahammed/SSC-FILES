Module 1 gaps in the PYQ set

Scope: syllabus Module I (Electric and magnetic fields in matter, 16 marks), with the Griffiths 5th ed. sections listed in the syllabus. The check compares the 39 questions in module-1-pyq.pdf against the concepts in source/books/index.md.

Status key: - Blind: no question in the set touches this concept, so a student who only studies these papers will not see it. - Partial: a question touches the concept but not the derivation, formula or worked case that the syllabus expects.

Summary of blind concepts

The most important blind concepts for a student who studies only these papers:

  1. The field of a uniformly polarized sphere, and the uniformly magnetized sphere (Ex. 4.2 and Ex. 6.1). These are the two worked examples most likely to be set.
  2. Bound volume charge ρ_b = −∇·P, and the bound-charge physical picture.
  3. The dielectric sphere in a uniform field, E = 3E₀/(κ+2), and the boundary-value method with dielectrics.
  4. Magnetic dipole torque and force (m × B and ∇(m·B)), and the energy U = −m·B.
  5. The solenoid filled with linear magnetic material.
  6. Diamagnetism from electron orbits (Larmor precession, Eq. 6.8).
  7. H and B boundary conditions. These were excluded by decision D3 and should be reconsidered.

Beyond the syllabus list (in the book, not in Module 1)

  • Macroscopic vs microscopic fields (4.2.3)
  • Curl of D and no Coulomb law for D (4.3.2)
  • Electrostatic force on a dielectric slab (4.4.3, pages 195–203)
  • Anisotropic susceptibility tensor (4.4.1)
  • ∇·H = −∇·M (6.3.2)

These are useful for depth but should not be the main focus of a Module 1 paper.
