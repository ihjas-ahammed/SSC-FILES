9.1.3 Reflection and transmission on a string

Source: book-sources/m3-sec2-bc.html, Griffiths pp. 396–399. Equations 9.21–9.33 in full. Background: beyond the syllabus list (items 9–14 use §9.1.1, §9.1.2 and §9.1.4). Problems 9.5–9.7 not worked.

Prerequisites

  • Sinusoidal waves and complex amplitudes (§9.1.2).
  • Continuity of a function and of its first derivative at a point.

1. Setup

Two strings of equal tension $T$ and mass densities $\mu_1$, $\mu_2$ are joined at $z=0$. Wave speeds: $v_1=\sqrt{T/\mu_1}$, $v_2=\sqrt{T/\mu_2}$.

2. Incident, reflected and transmitted waves

$$\tilde f_I=\tilde A_Ie^{i(k_1z-\omega t)}\quad(z<0)\tag{9.21}$$
$$\tilde f_R=\tilde A_Re^{i(-k_1z-\omega t)}\quad(z<0)\tag{9.22}$$
$$\tilde f_T=\tilde A_Te^{i(k_2z-\omega t)}\quad(z>0)\tag{9.23}$$

Common frequency $\omega$; wavelength ratio $\lambda_1/\lambda_2=k_2/k_1=v_1/v_2$ Eq. 9.24.

{{FIG:m3-sec2-pulse}}

A finite pulse is a superposition of sinusoids (Eq. 9.20); a single frequency requires infinite extent. The net disturbance is

$$\tilde f(z,t)=\begin{cases}\tilde A_Ie^{i(k_1z-\omega t)}+\tilde A_Re^{i(-k_1z-\omega t)}, & z<0\\ \tilde A_Te^{i(k_2z-\omega t)}, & z>0\end{cases}\tag{9.25}$$

3. Matching conditions

Result 9.3: junction conditions
$$f(0^-,t)=f(0^+,t)\tag{9.26}$$
$$\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\right|_{0^-}=\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\right|_{0^+}\tag{9.27}$$

Eq. 9.27 holds for a massless knot; a mismatch would give an infinite acceleration (Fig. 9.6). The same conditions hold for $\tilde f$ Eq. 9.28:

$$\tilde f(0^-,t)=\tilde f(0^+,t),\qquad\left.\frac{\partial\tilde f}{\partial z}\right|_{0^-}=\left.\frac{\partial\tilde f}{\partial z}\right|_{0^+}\tag{9.28}$$

4. Amplitudes

Continuity: $\tilde A_I+\tilde A_R=\tilde A_T$. Slope: $k_1(\tilde A_I-\tilde A_R)=k_2\tilde A_T$. Hence

$$\tilde A_R=\frac{k_1-k_2}{k_1+k_2}\tilde A_I,\qquad\tilde A_T=\frac{2k_1}{k_1+k_2}\tilde A_I\tag{9.29}$$

In terms of speeds (via 9.24):

$$\tilde A_R=\frac{v_2-v_1}{v_2+v_1}\tilde A_I,\qquad\tilde A_T=\frac{2v_2}{v_2+v_1}\tilde A_I\tag{9.30}$$

With real amplitudes and phases:

$$A_Re^{i\delta_R}=\frac{v_2-v_1}{v_2+v_1}A_Ie^{i\delta_I},\qquad A_Te^{i\delta_T}=\frac{2v_2}{v_2+v_1}A_Ie^{i\delta_I}\tag{9.31}$$

Case $v_2>v_1$: no phase change; $A_R=\dfrac{v_2-v_1}{v_2+v_1}A_I$, $A_T=\dfrac{2v_2}{v_2+v_1}A_I$ Eq. 9.32.

Case $v_2 reflected phase shifted by $\pi$ (“upside down”); $A_R=\dfrac{v_1-v_2}{v_2+v_1}A_I$, $A_T=\dfrac{2v_2}{v_2+v_1}A_I$ Eq. 9.33.

Fixed end ($v_2\to0$ relative): $A_R=A_I$, $A_T=0$, full reflection.
