$$B_z(x,y)=X(x)Y(y),$$

$$B_z=B_0\cos(m\pi x/a)\cos(n\pi y/b).\qquad(9.188)$$

$$k=\sqrt{(\omega/c)^2-\pi^2\left[(m/a)^2+(n/b)^2\right]}.\qquad(9.189)$$

9.5.3 The Coaxial Transmission Line

In Section 9.5.1 I showed that a hollow wave guide cannot support TEM waves. $$k=\omega/c,\qquad(9.195)$$
