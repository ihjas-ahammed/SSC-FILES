1. Magnetic charge (§7.3.4)

In free space ($\rho=0$, $\mathbf J=\mathbf 0$) the four equations are symmetric. Replacing $\mathbf E\to\mathbf B$ and $\mathbf B\to-\mu_0\epsilon_0\mathbf E$ swaps the two pairs. If magnetic charge density $\rho_m$ and magnetic current $\mathbf J_m$ existed, the full set would be (Eq. 7.53):

$$\begin{aligned}\nabla\cdot\mathbf E&=\frac{\rho_e}{\epsilon_0},\qquad\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=\mu_0\rho_m,\\\nabla\times\mathbf E&=-\mu_0\mathbf J_m-\frac{\partial\mathbf B}{\partial t},\qquad\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J_e+\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial\mathbf E}{\partial t}\end{aligned} \tag{7.53}$$

Each charge would be conserved (Eq. 7.54): $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_m=-\partial\rho_m/\partial t$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_e=-\partial\rho_e/\partial t$. The force between magnetic charges is Eq. 7.55, $\mathbf F=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{q_{m1}q_{m2}}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf r}$.

No magnetic monopole has been found, and Maxwell’s equations do not contain one. Magnetic charge would explain why electric charge is quantised (Dirac; Problem 8.21). Footnotes 31–33 give the search literature.

2. The rotating magnet (§7.4)

Faraday’s experiments with a disk and a magnet (Fig. 7.51) show that a magnet rotating about its own symmetry axis does not drag its field round. A magnet rotating about a perpendicular axis does drag its field along. In 1912 Barnett concluded that the Earth’s field, as associated with the Earth’s rotation about its axis, is not dragged.

In Maxwell’s theory the fields are the physical quantities, and field lines are a construct for drawing them. The labelling of field lines has no physical content (Fig. 7.52), so the statement that field lines rotate with their source is a choice of description, not a physical result. Only the magnitude and direction at each point matter.

The Lorentz force uses the charge’s velocity relative to a frame, which makes the magnetic case subtle. Footnote 36 corrects footnote 34: moving magnetic field lines do not induce electric fields; time-dependent fields do.
