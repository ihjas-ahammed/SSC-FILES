Pre-physics bridge: Griffiths §4.1 Polarization

Griffiths, Introduction to Electrodynamics (5th ed.), §4.1 (book pp. 166–172). Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) physics.

Most of §4.1 is built on Class 12 electrostatics. The list below is for recall: check each item against your Plus Two notes, and learn the items marked Beyond before the section.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two; learn the basic idea first.
