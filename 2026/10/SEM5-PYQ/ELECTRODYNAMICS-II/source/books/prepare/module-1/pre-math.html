Pre-math bridge: Griffiths §4.1 Polarization

Griffiths, Introduction to Electrodynamics (5th ed.), §4.1 (book pp. 166–172). Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) mathematics.

Most of the maths in §4.1 is in Plus Two: algebra, trigonometry, vectors, differentiation and integration. The items marked Beyond are the ones to learn before the section.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two; learn the basic idea first.
