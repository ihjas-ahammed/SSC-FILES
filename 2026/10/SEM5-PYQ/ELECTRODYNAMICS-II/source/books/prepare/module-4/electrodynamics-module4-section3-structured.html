13.1 EMF induced in a rotating coil

Source: book-sources/m4-s3-ac-emf.html, Murugeshan pp. 329–333 (printed). Syllabus item 17. The book does not number the equations of this section; they are shown unnumbered.

Prerequisites

  • Magnetic flux and Faraday’s law (pre-physics P4).
  • Sinusoidal functions and their integrals (pre-math M5, M7).

1. Set-up

A rectangular coil of $N$ turns, sides $a$ and $b$, rotates at angular velocity $\omega$ about an axis perpendicular to a uniform field $\mathbf B$ (Fig. 13.1). The normal makes angle $\theta=\omega t$ with $\mathbf B$ at time $t$, taking $\theta=0$ at the instant the coil plane is perpendicular to $\mathbf B$.

$$\phi = NBA\cos\omega t,\qquad A=ab$$

2. Induced emf

Recall (Plus Two) Flux $\phi=BA\cos\theta$ and emf $-d\phi/dt$ (Faraday). With $N$ turns and $\theta=\omega t$ the emf is sinusoidal.
$$E = -\frac{d\phi}{dt} = E_0\sin\omega t,\qquad E_0 = NBA\omega$$

$E_0$ is the peak value. The period is $T=1/\nu$ and $\omega=2\pi\nu$ (Fig. 13.3).

3. Mean, r.m.s. and virtual values

$$I_{mean} = \frac{2I_0}{\pi} = 0.637\,I_0,\qquad E_{mean} = 0.637\,E_0$$
$$\begin{gathered}\overline{I^2} = \frac{I_0^2}{2} \\ I_{rms} = \frac{I_0}{\sqrt2} = 0.707\,I_0 \\ E_{rms} = \frac{E_0}{\sqrt 2}\end{gathered}$$

The r.m.s. value equals the virtual (effective) value: the direct current giving the same heat in the same resistance.

4. Form factor and half-cycle mean

$$\begin{aligned}\text{Form factor} &= \frac{I_{rms}}{I_{mean}} \\ &= \frac{0.707}{0.637} \\ &= 1.11\end{aligned}$$

Over half a period, $E_{av} = 2E_0/\pi$.

5. Impedance and reactance

The impedance is $Z = E/I$; reactance is the part due to $L$ or $C$ ($X_L$, $X_C$); admittance is $Y=1/Z$.
