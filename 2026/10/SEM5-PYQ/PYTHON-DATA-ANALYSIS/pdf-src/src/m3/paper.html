Units 11–16 · syllabus 2024 · module marks 15, doubled to 30
How this paper works

The syllabus gives this module 15 marks. This paper gives twice that (30 marks). Plots are drawn from the physics data named in the syllabus: capacitors, the convex-lens liquid-lens experiment, the sonometer, and the Iris data set.

No past paper of this exact course was found, so the questions use similar CUFYUGP papers. PYQ = adapted from a similar paper, named with its code, term and question number. Instructor = written from the syllabus, with no close match.

Similar papers used: MAT4CJ205 Python and SageMath (Apr 2026); STA3MN201 Statistical Inference using R (Nov 2025); STA2MN101 Probability Theory I (Apr 2025).
