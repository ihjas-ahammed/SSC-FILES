Continuous Functions

Bartle & Sherbert §5.1, §5.3 (5.3.1–5.3.5, 5.3.7–5.3.10), §5.4 (5.4.1–5.4.14) · FYUGP pattern (April 2026), doubled
How this paper works

A real FYUGP paper has 10 short (3 marks), 8 paragraph (6 marks) and 2 essay (10 marks, answer one) questions. This paper has twice as many (20 + 16 + 4), all from Module 1. Each of the module's 40 concepts is the main point of exactly one question.

Time4 hours (or two sittings of 2 hours) Maximum140 marks (A ceiling 48, B ceiling 72, C answer any two)

PYQ MTS-25 Q9 = MTS 6B 10, March 2025, question 9 (old CBCSS course, same textbook). "also" = another paper asked the same thing. "adapted" = a 1-mark MCQ turned into a 3-mark question. CALC-24 = related FYUGP Calculus paper (Nov 2024).

27.
Let $f : [a,b] \to \mathbb R$ be continuous and $\inf f(I) \le k \le \sup f(I)$. Prove that $f(c) = k$ for some $c \in I$. Illustrate with $f(x) = x^3 - 3x$ on [−1.5, 1.5] and k = 1.
PYQ MAT-22 Q13
28.
Prove that the image of a closed bounded interval under a continuous function is a closed bounded interval. Show by an example that it need not be $[f(a), f(b)]$.
PYQ MAT-21 Q15
29.
State and prove the Preservation of Intervals Theorem. Find $f((-1,1))$ and $f([0,\infty))$ for $f(x) = \frac{1}{x^2+1}$.
PYQ MTS-25 Q17 also MAT-22 Q14
30.
State the Nonuniform Continuity Criteria. Show that $1/x$ is not uniformly continuous on $(0,\infty)$. Is it uniformly continuous on $[a, 1)$, $a > 0$?
PYQ MAT-21 Q16 also MTS-22 MCQ 2
31.
Prove that a Lipschitz function on A is uniformly continuous on A. Find δ for $\sin x$ on ℝ.
PYQ MAT-21 Q28b
32.
Prove that $g(x) = \sqrt x$ is uniformly continuous on $[0, \infty)$ (in particular on [0, 2]).
PYQ MTS-26 Q24b also MAT-22 Q36b
33.
Show that if f and g are uniformly continuous on ℝ, then $f + g$ is, but the product $fg$ need not be.
PYQ MAT-21 Q22
34.
If f is uniformly continuous on A and $(x_n)$ is a Cauchy sequence in A, prove that $(f(x_n))$ is Cauchy. Show this can fail for a function that is merely continuous.
PYQ MAT-21 Q36a also MTS-22 Q3
35.
Let f be continuous on [a, b] and ε > 0. Prove that there is a step function $s_\varepsilon$ with $|f(x) - s_\varepsilon(x)| < \varepsilon$ on [a, b]. Find m and $s_\varepsilon$ for $f(x) = x$ on [0, 1], ε = 0.1.
PYQ MTS-26 Q17
36.
Define a piecewise linear function. Prove that a continuous f on [a, b] can be approximated within ε by a continuous piecewise linear function. For $x^2$ on [0, 1] with nodes 0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1, find the largest error.
