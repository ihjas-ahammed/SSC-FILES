How to study with it

From research on how strong students learn analysis.

  1. Do, don't re-read. Re-reading and highlighting barely help. Closing the file and writing a definition from memory helps a lot.
  2. Explain every line. For each proof step, say why it is true (a definition, an earlier line, or a named theorem). The grey "why" lines show you how.
  3. Skeleton first. In the exam, write the skeleton, leave a gap, and fill the gap last.
  4. Know the counterexamples. Half of analysis is knowing which picture breaks a wrong idea.
  5. Little and often. Use the recall plan on the last pages: today, in 1, 3, 7 and 14 days.
