How this paper works

A real FYUGP paper has 10 short (3 marks), 8 paragraph (6 marks) and 2 essay (10 marks, answer one) questions. This paper has twice as many (20 + 16 + 4), all from Module 4, and every concept of the module is the main point of at least one question.

Time4 hours (or two sittings of 2 hours) Maximum140 marks (A ceiling 48, B ceiling 72, C answer any two)

Section B · Paragraph / problemAll questions can be answered. Each carries 6 marks. Ceiling 72.

PYQ MAT-21 Q21

PYQ MAT-21 Q32

PYQ MAT-22 Q31
24.

25.
For $g_n(x) = x^n$ on [0, 1], show that each $g_n$ has a continuous derivative, that $(g_n)$ converges pointwise, and that the limit is neither continuous nor differentiable at 1. Why does uniform convergence fail?
26.
Let $f_n(x) = \dfrac{nx}{1+n^2x^2}$ on $[0, \infty)$. Show $f_n \to 0$ pointwise, find $\lVert f_n\rVert$, and decide whether the convergence is uniform on $[0, \infty)$ and on $[a, \infty)$, $a > 0$.
27.
Let $f_n$ (n ≥ 2) be 0 on $[2/n, 1]$ and have a triangular graph with vertices (0, 0), (1/n, n), (2/n, 0) on [0, 2/n]. Show $f_n \to 0$ pointwise and $\int_0^1 f_n = 1$. What does this show?
28.
Show that $\frac{x^2+nx}{n} \to x$ uniformly on [0, 8] and on every interval $[-b, b]$, but not on ℝ.
29.
Let $f_n(x) = x + \frac1n$. Show $f_n \to x$ uniformly on ℝ, that $f_n^2$ does not converge uniformly to $x^2$ on ℝ, but that it does on [0, 1].
30.
Find the pointwise limit of $f_n(x) = \dfrac{1}{1+x^n}$ on [0, 1]. Is the convergence uniform? Find $\lVert f_n - f\rVert$.
31.
Using the Cauchy criterion, show that $(x^n)$ does not converge uniformly on [0, 1], without finding its limit.
32.
Show that $F_n(x) = \frac1n \sin nx \to 0$ uniformly on ℝ, but that $(F_n')$ does not converge. What does this show?
33.
Show that $x^2 e^{-nx} \to 0$ uniformly on $[0, \infty)$ and find the norm.
34.
Show that $n^2x^2 e^{-nx} \to 0$ pointwise on $[0, \infty)$, not uniformly, but uniformly on $[a, \infty)$ for $a>0$.
35.
Let $f_n(x) = \dfrac{nx}{1+nx}$ on [0, 1]. Find the limit f, show the convergence is not uniform, and yet $\int_0^1 f_n \to \int_0^1 f$.
36.
If $f_n \to f$ and $g_n \to g$ uniformly on A, prove $f_n + g_n \to f + g$ uniformly. If, in addition, all $f_n, g_n$ are bounded, prove $f_ng_n \to fg$ uniformly.
