Differentiation

Bartle & Sherbert §6.1 (6.1.1–6.1.7), §6.2 (6.2.1–6.2.13) · FYUGP pattern (April 2026), doubled
How this paper works

A real FYUGP paper has 10 short (3 marks), 8 paragraph (6 marks) and 2 essay (10 marks, answer one) questions. This paper has twice as many (20 + 16 + 4), all from Module 2. Each of the module's 24 concepts is the main point of exactly one question; 16 of the finer points get a question of their own.

Time4 hours (or two sittings of 2 hours) Maximum140 marks (A ceiling 48, B ceiling 72, C answer any two)

No old Calicut paper has asked a Module 2 question (differentiation used to be taught in an earlier semester). So the tags are: CALC-24 Q5 = related FYUGP Calculus paper (MAT1MN102, Nov 2024), question 5; PYQ MTS-22 MCQ 5 = MTS 6B 10, March 2022, MCQ 5, "adapted" = a 1-mark MCQ turned into a 3-mark question; Bartle Ex 6.1.1(b) = a starred exercise of the textbook; Bartle Thm 6.2.4 = a theorem of the textbook. Unless a question says otherwise, you may quote the Plus Two derivatives ($\sin' = \cos$, $\cos' = -\sin$, $(e^x)' = e^x$, $(\ln x)' = 1/x$).

Section A · Short answerAll questions can be answered. Each carries 3 marks. Ceiling 48.
1.
Define the derivative of $f : I \to \mathbb R$ at $c \in I$ using $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$. Use your definition to verify that the derivative of $f(x) = x^2$ at $c = 2$ is 4.
Bartle Def 6.1.1
2.
Using the definition of the derivative, find $f'(c)$ for $f(x) = 3x$, $x \in \mathbb R$.
CALC-24 Q5 · related FYUGP
3.
From the definition, find the derivative of $g(x) = 1/x$ at a point $c \ne 0$.
Bartle Ex 6.1.1(b)
4.
If $f$ is differentiable at $c$, show that $f'(c) = \lim_{n\to\infty} n\{f(c + 1/n) - f(c)\}$. Show by $f(x) = |x|$ that the converse is false.
Bartle Ex 6.1.13
5.
Show that $f(x) = x^{1/3}$ is continuous but not differentiable at 0. What does the graph do at 0?
Bartle Ex 6.1.2
6.
State the quotient rule and use it to differentiate $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{1+x^2}$.
Bartle Ex 6.1.5(a)
7.
Prove by induction, using the product rule, that $D\,x^n = n x^{n-1}$ for $n \in \mathbb N$. Deduce $D\,x^{-n} = -n x^{-n-1}$.
Bartle Cor 6.1.4
8.
State the chain rule. Differentiate $g(x) = \sqrt{x^2 - 2x + 5}$ and say why it is differentiable for every $x \in \mathbb R$.
Bartle Ex 6.1.5(b)
9.
Let $f : \mathbb R \to \mathbb R$ be differentiable. Prove that $f'$ is odd if $f$ is even, and $f'$ is even if $f$ is odd.
Bartle Ex 6.1.9
10.
If $f$ is differentiable at $c$ and $f(c) \ne 0$, find $|f|'(c)$. If $f(c) = 0$, when is $|f|$ differentiable at $c$? Find $|f|'$ for $f(x) = x^2 - 1$ and the points where it fails.
Bartle Ex 6.1.7
11.
Show that $h(x) = x|x|$ is differentiable at every $x \in \mathbb R$ and find $h'$.
Bartle Ex 6.1.8(c)
12.
Using the quotient rule, $\sin' = \cos$ and $\cos' = -\sin$, prove $D\tan x = \sec^2 x$ and $D\sec x = \sec x \tan x$. State where they hold.
Bartle 6.1.7(d)
13.
For $r > 0$ let $f(x) = x^r\sin(1/x)$ for $x \ne 0$, $f(0) = 0$. For which $r$ does $f'(0)$ exist?
Bartle Ex 6.1.12
14.
Find all critical points of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x + 1$. Which of them can be relative extrema, and why? State the theorem you use.
CALC-24 Q10 · related FYUGP
15.
Find the relative extrema of $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2}{x^4 + 16}$ using the First Derivative Test.
CALC-24 Q18 · related FYUGP
16.
Verify the Mean Value Theorem for $f(x) = x^3$ on $[0, 2]$ and find the number $c$.
Bartle Thm 6.2.4
17.
Define "increasing" and "strictly increasing" for $f : I \to \mathbb R$ (as in Bartle). Give an example of a strictly increasing function whose derivative is 0 at a point.
CALC-24 Q9 · related FYUGP
18.
Find the intervals on which $f(x) = x^4 - 5x^3 + 9x^2$ is increasing and decreasing.
CALC-24 Q20(1) · related FYUGP
19.
Using the sign of the derivative, decide which of the following are increasing on $(0, 1)$: (i) $1 - x^3$, (ii) $x^2 + x$, (iii) $1/x^2$.
PYQ MTS-22 MCQ 5 · adapted
20.
State Lemma 6.2.11 (the sign of $f(x) - f(c)$ near $c$ when $f'(c) \ne 0$) and prove part (a).
Bartle Lemma 6.2.11
Section B · Paragraph / problemAll questions can be answered. Each carries 6 marks. Ceiling 72.
21.
Prove that a function differentiable at $c$ is continuous at $c$. Show by an example that the converse is false.
Bartle Thm 6.1.2 related MAT-22 MCQ 4, MAT-21 MCQ 3 ($\lim |x|/x$)
22.
Let $f(x) = x^2$ for $x \le 1$ and $f(x) = 4\sqrt x$ for $x > 1$. (a) Is $f$ differentiable at 1? Justify. (b) Find constants $a, b$ such that $F(x) = x^2$ ($x \le 1$), $F(x) = ax + b$ ($x > 1$) is differentiable at 1. (c) Is $G(x) = x^2$ ($x \le 1$), $G(x) = \sqrt x$ ($x > 1$) differentiable at 1?
CALC-24 Q13 · related FYUGP
23.
State and prove the product rule for derivatives at a point. Deduce $(1/g)'(c) = -g'(c)/g(c)^2$ when $g(c) \ne 0$.
Bartle Thm 6.1.3(c)
24.
State and prove Carathéodory's Theorem. Find $\varphi$ for $f(x) = x^3$ at $c$ and deduce $f'(c)$. Explain why $|x|$ has no such $\varphi$ at 0.
Bartle Thm 6.1.5
25.
Let $f(x) = x^2\sin(1/x)$ for $x \ne 0$, $f(0) = 0$. Show that $f$ is differentiable at every point, find $f'$, and show that $f'$ is not continuous at 0.
Bartle Ex 6.1.7(e)
26.
State and prove the Interior Extremum Theorem. Show by examples that "interior", "differentiable" and the converse cannot be dropped.
Bartle Thm 6.2.1
27.
State and prove Rolle's Theorem. Verify it for $f(x) = x(x-1)(x-2)$ on $[0, 2]$.
Bartle Thm 6.2.3
28.
Prove that a function continuous on $[a, b]$ with $f' = 0$ on $(a, b)$ is constant. Deduce that two such functions with equal derivatives differ by a constant. Why does the argument fail on $(0,1) \cup (2,3)$?
Bartle Thm 6.2.5, Cor 6.2.6
29.
State and prove part (a) of the First Derivative Test. Apply it to $f(x) = x^3 - 3x$, and to $f(x) = |x|$ at 0.
Bartle Thm 6.2.8
30.
(a) Use Rolle's Theorem to show that between two consecutive zeros of $\sin x$ there is a zero of $\cos x$. (b) Show that $p(x) = x^3 + 3x + 1$ has exactly one real root and locate it in an interval of length $\frac14$.
Bartle 6.2.9(a) related MAT-21 Q17
31.
Use the Mean Value Theorem for $f(x) = \sqrt x$ on $[100, 105]$ to show $10.2272 < \sqrt{105} < 10.25$, and then sharpen the lower bound.
Bartle 6.2.9(b)
32.
Using the Mean Value Theorem prove (a) $e^x \ge 1 + x$ for all $x \in \mathbb R$, with equality only at $x = 0$; (b) $\dfrac{x-1}{x} < \ln x < x - 1$ for $x > 1$.
Bartle 6.2.10(a), Ex 6.2.7
33.
Prove (a) $-x \le \sin x \le x$ for $x \ge 0$; (b) $|\sin x - \sin y| \le |x - y|$ for all $x, y$; (c) if $f$ is differentiable on an interval $I$ and $|f'| \le K$ on $I$, then $f$ is Lipschitz.
Bartle 6.2.10(b), Ex 6.2.15 related MTS-22 MCQ 4
34.
If $\alpha > 1$, prove Bernoulli's inequality $(1+x)^\alpha \ge 1 + \alpha x$ for all $x > -1$, with equality if and only if $x = 0$.
Bartle 6.2.10(c)
35.
Let $0 < \alpha < 1$. Show $x^\alpha \le \alpha x + (1 - \alpha)$ for $x \ge 0$, and deduce $a^\alpha b^{1-\alpha} \le \alpha a + (1-\alpha) b$ for $a, b > 0$. Hence prove $\sqrt{ab} \le \frac{a+b}{2}$.
Bartle 6.2.10(d)
36.
Show that $g : [-1, 1] \to \mathbb R$ with $g = -1$ on $[-1, 0)$, $g(0) = 0$, $g = 1$ on $(0, 1]$ is not the derivative of any function. Give two functions on $\mathbb R$, not differing by a constant, whose derivative is $h(x) = 0$ ($x < 0$), $h(x) = 1$ ($x > 0$) for all $x \ne 0$.
Bartle Cor 6.2.13, Ex 6.2.12
Section C · EssayAnswer any two. Each carries 10 marks.
37.
State and prove the Chain Rule using Carathéodory's Theorem. Explain why the naive proof (dividing by $f(x) - f(c)$) fails. Use the rule to differentiate $\sin(x^2)$ and $|x^2 - 1|$.
Bartle Thm 6.1.6
38.
State and prove the Mean Value Theorem. Give its geometric meaning and show, with examples, that neither continuity on $[a, b]$ nor differentiability on $(a, b)$ can be dropped.
Bartle Thm 6.2.4
39.
State and prove Darboux's Theorem (you may use Lemma 6.2.11). Treat both orderings $f'(a) < k < f'(b)$ and $f'(a) > k > f'(b)$.
Bartle Thm 6.2.12
40.
Let $f$ be differentiable on an interval $I$. Prove: $f$ is increasing if and only if $f' \ge 0$ on $I$. Show that $f' > 0$ implies strictly increasing but not conversely, and that $f'(c) > 0$ at a single point does not give monotonicity near $c$. Apply the result to $f(x) = x - \sin x$.
Bartle Thm 6.2.7, Ex 6.2.10, 6.2.13
