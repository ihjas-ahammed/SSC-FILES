The Riemann Integral

Bartle & Sherbert §7.1 (7.1.1–7.1.7), §7.2 (7.2.1–7.2.13), §7.3 (7.3.1–7.3.18) · FYUGP pattern (April 2026), doubled
How this paper works

A real FYUGP paper has 10 short (3 marks, ceiling 24), 8 paragraph (6 marks, ceiling 36) and 2 essay (10 marks, answer one) questions. This paper has twice as many (20 + 16 + 4 = 40 questions, 196 marks), all from Module 3. Each of the module's 32 atomic concepts (T01–T32) and 8 core application types is the main point of exactly one question.

Time4 hours (or two sittings of 2 hours) Maximum140 marks (A ceiling 48, B ceiling 72, C answer any two = 20)

What happens if f(x) = 0 except at finitely many points, or if f(x) = g(x) off the infinite set \{1/n : n \in \mathbb{N}\}?

If J is a subinterval of [a,b] with endpoints c < d and \varphi_J(x) = 1 on J, 0 elsewhere on [a,b], prove that \varphi_J \in \mathcal{R}[a,b] and \int_a^b \varphi_J = d - c. Hence evaluate \int_0^4 g where g(x) = 3 for 0 \le x \le 2 and g(x) = 2 for 2 < x \le 4.
PYQ MAT-21 MCQ 10 · adapted
9.

10.
