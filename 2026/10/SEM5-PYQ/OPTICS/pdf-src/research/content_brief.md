## 2. DERIVATION SKELETONS
D1 Optical path/Fermat (§3.1): 1) speed=c/n ⇒ dt=n ds/c; 2) sum ⇒(3.1); 3) c const ⇒ extremal ∫n ds is ray (3.2); 4) first variation zero (3.3).
D2 Rectilinear: 1) n const ⇒ ∫n ds=nL; 2) straight line shortest ⇒ minimum; 3) nearby AEB, ADB longer.
D3 Reflection (Fig 3.4): 1) A′ on ⟂ from A with AR=RA′, AP=PA′; 2) APB=A′PB length; 3) minimum ⇒P on straight A′B; 4) A,A′,P,B coplanar, normal PS in plane; 5) geometry ∠APS=∠SPB, i=r; 6) also straight AB is a ray (two rays A→B).
D4 Snell (Fig 3.5): 1) AM=h1,BN=h2,MR=x,MN=L fixed; 2) L_op=n1AR+n2RB; 3) (3.4) Pythagoras; 4) dL/dx=0 ⇒(3.5) n1x/√(x²+h1²)−n2(L−x)/√((L−x)²+h2²)=0; 5) sinθ1=x/√, sinθ2=(L−x)/√; 6) (3.6).
D5 Paraboloid (Ex 3.1): 1) QL⟂directrix AB, QL=QS (parabola); 2) PQ+QS=PQ+QL; 3) minimum ⇒ P,Q,L collinear; 4) PQ∥axis; all ∥ rays→S, converse too.
D6 Ellipse (Ex 3.2): S1P+S2P const ⇒ stationary (neither max nor min); corollaries: only axial rays pass through another axis point Q; valid for ellipsoid of revolution.
D7 Spherical surface Fermat (Ex 3.3, Fig 3.10; R>0 if C right of P, x,y,r magnitudes): 1) OS=[(x+r)²+r²−2(x+r)r cosθ]^½; 2) cosθ≈1−θ²/2; 3) OS≈x+½r²(1/r+1/x)θ² (binomial); 4) SQ≈y−½r²(1/r−1/y)θ²; 5) L=n1OS+n2SQ ⇒(3.7); 6) dL/dθ=0 ⇒(3.8) r²[n1/x+n2/y−(n2−n1)/r]θ=0; 7) θ=0 only axial ray OPQ (normal incidence, undeviated) unless bracket=0; 8) bracket=0 ⇒(3.9) all paraxial rays equal time ⇒ image at y0; 9) u=−x,v=+y,r=+R ⇒(3.10)=(4.5).
D8 Nature of extremum: d²L/dθ²=r²[n1/x+n2/y−(n2−n1)/r]=r²n2(1/y−1/y0); y>y0 max, y<y0 min; also n1OP+n2PI=n1OS+n2SI, so n1OP+n2PQ=n1OS+n2(SI+IQ)>n1OS+n2SQ (Q right of I ⇒ max).
D9 Virtual image (Ex 3.4): 1) require n1OS−n2SI θ-independent (3.11); 2) L=(n1OS−n2SI)+n2(IS+SP); 3) 2nd bracket extremal ⇒ straight line ⇒ refracted ray appears to come from I (3.12).
D10 Mirror by Fermat (Pr 3.1/3.2): L=OS+SQ (3.90) stationary ⇒1/x+1/y=2/r. Object inside r/2 (N20-Q25, Pr 3.3, no printed answer; DERIVED): Q behind mirror, SQ≈y+½r²(1/y+1/r)θ²; L=OS−SQ≈x−y+½r²[1/x−1/y−2/r]θ²; dL/dθ=0 ⇒1/x−1/y=2/r; y>0 iff x<r/2 (virtual, behind); sign form u=−x,v=+y,R=−r ⇒1/u+1/v=2/R.
D11 Surface by geometry (Fig 4.1a): 1) (4.1) with sinφ≈φ; 2) φ1=β+α1, φ2=β−α2; 3) (4.2),(4.3) with β≈h/r, α1≈h/x, α2≈h/y, OD≈x, ID≈y; 4) n1φ1=n2φ2 ⇒(4.4); 5) u=−x,v=y,R=r ⇒(4.5). Same ray in G's slope-angle form: θ̂1=α1, θ̂2=−α2 (convention item 3).
D12 Mirror (Fig 4.3): 1) φ1=β−α1≈h/r−h/x; φ2=α2−β≈h/y−h/r; 2) φ1=φ2; 3)(4.6); 4) u=−x,v=−y,R=−r ⇒(4.7). Check: (4.5) with n2=−n1: −n1/v−n1/u=−2n1/R ⇒(4.7).
D13 Thin lens: 1) surface1 (4.8); 2) Q=object for surface 2 ⇒(4.9) (n1,n2 swapped); 3) distances from lens centre P (t≈0); 4) add: v′ cancels ⇒ n1/v−n1/u=(n2−n1)(1/R1−1/R2); 5) ÷n1 ⇒(4.10); 6) define f ⇒(4.11),(4.12).
D14 General foci: 1) add (4.8)-type and n3/v−n2/v′=(n3−n2)/R2 ⇒(4.14); 2) v=∞,u=f1 ⇒(4.15); 3) u=−∞,v=f2 ⇒(4.16); 4) n3=n1 ⇒(4.13) f1=−f2=f.
D15 Newton (Fig 4.8a): 1) similar triangles at F1 ⇒(4.17); 2) at F2 ⇒(4.18); 3) equate ⇒f1f2=x1x2 (4.19); 4) f1=−f,f2=f ⇒x1x2=−f² (4.20). Equivalent: u=x1+f1,v=x2+f2 (G eq 4.21 usage).
D16 Magnification: lens (4.21) from (4.17),(4.18), v=f2+x2,u=f1+x1. Surface (Fig 4.9): 1) (4.22) triangles AOC,ICB; 2) rewrite (4.5): n2/n1−v/u=((n2−n1)/n1)(v/R); u/v−n1/n2=((n2−n1)/n2)(u/R); 3) substitute ⇒(4.23) (algebra checked: ratio=−n1v/(n2u)); 4) m1m2 ⇒(4.24)=v/u.
D17 Longitudinal (derived): 1/v−1/u=1/f ⇒ −dv/v²+du/u²=0 ⇒dv/du=v²/u²=m².
FLAGS vs NCERT: (a) Ghatak is fixed left-to-right convention (same as NCERT Cartesian for u,v,R,f; mirror 1/u+1/v=2/R equals NCERT 1/v+1/u=1/f, f=R/2; concave R<0). (b) Ghatak's mirror/surface derivations first use magnitudes x,y,r, then substitute signs. (c) Surface eq. is n2/v−n1/u; the "n1/u+n2/v=(n2−n1)/R" form (real-is-positive) used in U practice Pr p.cu.op.50 and in the N19-Q18 topic note is NOT Ghatak. (d) Ghatak defines two focal lengths f1,f2 (f1=−f in air, f1<0 for converging); NCERT has one f. (e) Newton: Ghatak x1x2=−f² with signed x1 from F1 (x1<0 left), NCERT xx′=f² magnitudes. (f) n=n2/n1 in (4.10),(4.12) is relative index. (g) Ex 4.2 prints convex mirror as R=−20 with u=−8 (book's own frame after reflection); reproduce as printed. (h) Ghatak calls (4.10) "thin lens formula", usually written (4.11).
