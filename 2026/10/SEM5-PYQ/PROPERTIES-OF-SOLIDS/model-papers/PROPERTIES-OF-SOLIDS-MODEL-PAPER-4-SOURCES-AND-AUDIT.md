Numerical audit Q2: Curie law gives chi(300 K)=2.8e-4*(350/300)=3.2666667e-4. Q15: epsilon0 E=1.77e-6 C/m^2; chi_e=P/(epsilon0 E)=2; epsilon_r=3; D=epsilon0 E+P=5.31e-6 C/m^2.

Theory audit: diamagnetic induced response opposes the field; classical paramagnetic susceptibility is proportional to 1/T in weak fields; Weiss susceptibility above the ordering temperature follows C/(T-theta). For magnetite, A-site Fe3+ and B-site Fe3+ moments cancel and B-site Fe2+ leaves the net moment. Lorentz field has a positive P/(3 epsilon0) cavity term at cubic sites. Electronic polarisation survives to optical frequencies away from resonances; orientation relaxes first, then ionic. Absence of inversion is necessary, not alone sufficient, for piezoelectricity. Ferroelectric polarisation must be electrically switchable. No inaccurate numerical BaTiO3 transition temperature from the old book is copied into this paper.
