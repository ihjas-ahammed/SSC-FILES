Plus Two recall: standing waves between fixed ends

Recall. Standing waves on a string of length $L$ with fixed ends have $\lambda_n=2L/n$.

Re-derive.

  1. Displacement $\propto\sin(n\pi x/L)$ satisfies the end conditions.
  2. $k=n\pi/L$; with $p=\hbar k$ and $E=p^2/2m$, $E_n=\hbar^2n^2\pi^2/2mL^2$.

Saved. Eq. (5.10).

Inside the box, (5.2) reduces to (5.3), with general solution (5.4) and $k$ from (5.5). The conditions (5.6) follow from the infinite walls: $\psi_n(0)=\psi_n(L)=0$. They give $B=0$ (5.7) and $k=n\pi/L$ (5.8), so $\psi_n=A\sin(n\pi x/L)$ (5.9) and

The quantum number $n$ takes integer values. The spacing shrinks as $L$ grows (Fig. 5.3).

Normalisation: $A^2\int_0^L\sin^2(n\pi x/L)\,dx=1$ gives $A=\sqrt{2/L}$ and

$$\psi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2}{L}}\sin\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}x\right)\tag{5.11}$$

5.2.2 Fermi energy

Two electrons per level; $N$ electrons fill levels up to $n_F=N/2$ (5.12). The Fermi energy (5.13):

$$E_F=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(\frac{N\pi}{2L}\right)^2\tag{5.13}$$

Worked example in the book: $N/L=0.5$ Å$^{-1}$ gives $E_F=2.4$ eV.

5.2.3 Total energy

$$E_0=2\sum_{n=1}^{N/2}E_n=2\cdot\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(\frac{\pi}{L}\right)^2\sum_{n=1}^{N/2}n^2\tag{5.14}$$

With $\sum n^2\simeq\tfrac13(N/2)^3$, $E_0=\tfrac13NE_F$ (5.15). The average kinetic energy is one third of $E_F$.

5.2.4 Density of states

$D(E)=dn/dE$ (5.16) with the factor 2 for spin (5.17). Using $dE/dn=\hbar^2n/4mL^2$ and eliminating $n$:

$$D(E)=\frac{4L}{h}\left(\frac{m}{2E}\right)^{1/2}\tag{5.18}$$
{{FIG:m2-s2-dos1d}}
Result

1D: $E_n\propto n^2$; $E_0=\tfrac13 NE_F$; $D(E)\propto E^{-1/2}$; all states below $E_F$ filled at 0 K.
