### 4. Short notes

- **Source substitution (Book 2 not available):** The CU-FYUGP syllabus cites Book 2 (Subrahmanyam, Brij Lal & Avadhanulu) for Module 4 (items 17–22), which is not available on disk. Following `map.md`, all six units are authored from Ajoy Ghatak, *Optics* (6th ed., Book 1), Chapter 22 (§§22.1–22.3, 22.5–22.8, PDF pp. 355–360 and 365–375), using Ghatak's notation, sign conventions, and worked examples, and noting `"Ghatak ch. 22 (Book 2 not available)"` in every sheet's `source` field.
- **What was written:**
  - **13 glossary entries** (`optics-m4-…`) with plain-language definitions, aliases, and examples.
  - **2 sections, 6 topic sheets, and 59 cards** covering all six syllabus items (Units 17–22):
    1. `optics-m4-polarisation-introduction` (Unit 17, §22.1): linearly and circularly polarised waves on a string, derivation of the circular trajectory $x^2 + y^2 = a^2$, unpolarised light, Polaroid pass axis, and transverse electromagnetic waves ($\mathbf{E} \perp \mathbf{H} \perp \hat{z}$).
    2. `optics-m4-production-linearly-polarised` (Unit 18, §22.3): wire-grid polariser and Polaroid sheets, Brewster's law ($\theta_p = \tan^{-1}(n_2/n_1)$) and derivation of $\theta_p + \theta_r = 90^\circ$, dichroism (tourmaline), Nicol prism (Canada balsam total internal reflection), and polarisation by $90^\circ$ scattering.
    3. `optics-m4-malus-law-polariser-analyser` (Unit 19, §22.2): polariser and analyser setup, derivation of Malus's law $I = I_0\cos^2\theta$, parallel/45°/crossed Polaroids, and a 101-point JSXGraph intensity curve $I(\theta) = I_0\cos^2\theta$.
    4. `optics-m4-double-refraction-huygens` (Unit 20, §22.5): double refraction in calcite, ordinary and extraordinary rays, optic axis, negative vs. positive uniaxial crystals, derivation of the extraordinary ray velocity ellipsoid and ray refractive index $n_{re} = \sqrt{n_o^2\cos^2\theta + n_e^2\sin^2\theta}$, and Huygens' construction for normal and oblique incidence.
    5. `optics-m4-wave-plates` (Unit 21, §22.6): quarter-wave plate (QWP), derivation of phase difference $\theta = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda_0}(n_o - n_e)d$ and QWP thickness $d = (2m+1)\frac{\lambda_0}{4(n_o - n_e)}$, half-wave plate (HWP), and worked calcite QWP/HWP examples.
    6. `optics-m4-analysis-polarised-light` (Unit 22, §§22.7–22.8): systematic procedure to identify all seven states of polarisation using a rotating Polaroid and a QWP, optical activity, dextro- and laevo-rotatory media, and derivation of optical rotation $\phi(z) = \frac{\pi}{\lambda}(n_L - n_R)z$ from RCP and LCP normal modes.
