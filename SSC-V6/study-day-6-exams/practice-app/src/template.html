Quick guide

Press = on a card to open. In a numerical answer box, type = or start an expression with it. Press Enter to calculate. “Use answer” copies a real number back to that box. Escape closes.

Angles: RAD is the default. Toggle DEG for plain-number trigonometry and inverse trig. An explicit unit always works: sin(30 deg).
