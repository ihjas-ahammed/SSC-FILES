1Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. For $a, b \in G$, define $a \sim_R b$ if and only if $ab^{-1} \in H$. Prove that $\sim_R$ is an equivalence relation on $G$.3
2Write all the left cosets of the subgroup $3\mathbb{Z}$ in the additive group $\mathbb{Z}$, and explain why they form a partition of $\mathbb{Z}$.3
3Let $G = \mathbb{Z}_{12}$ under addition modulo $12$, and let $H$ be the subgroup $\langle 4 \rangle$. Find all distinct cosets of $H$ in $G$ and write down the index $(G : H)$.3
4Define the index of a subgroup $H$ in a group $G$. Find: (a) the index of $A_n$ in $S_n$ for $n > 1$, and (b) the index of $5\mathbb{Z}$ in $(\mathbb{Z}, +)$.3
5Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$ and let $a, b \in G$. Prove that if $aH = bH$, then $Ha^{-1} = Hb^{-1}$.3
6Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. Prove that for any $a \in G$, the left coset $aH$ has the same cardinality as $H$.3
7Prove that any group of prime order is cyclic and hence abelian.3
8Suppose $G$ is a group of order $31$. Can $G$ have any subgroup other than $\{e\}$ and $G$? Up to isomorphism, how many groups of order $31$ exist? Justify your answer.3
9Show that if a finite group $G$ with identity $e$ has order $n$, then $a^n = e$ for all $a \in G$. Hence calculate the remainder obtained when $45^{72}$ is divided by $73$.3
10Does there exist a subgroup of a group of order $6$ whose left cosets give a partition of the group into: (a) $6$ cells, (b) $4$ cells, (c) $12$ cells? Give an example if possible, or explain why it is impossible.3

11Let $G = \mathbb{Z}_{24}$ under addition modulo $24$ and let $H = \langle [3] \rangle$. List the elements of $H$, find all the cosets of $H$ in $G$, and state the index of $H$ in $\mathbb{Z}_{24}$.6
12Let $\sigma = (1, 2, 5, 4)(2, 3)$ be a permutation in $S_5$. Express $\sigma$ as a product of disjoint cycles, find the order of $\langle \sigma \rangle$, and find the index of the subgroup $\langle \sigma \rangle$ in $S_5$.6
13Find all the left cosets and all the right cosets of the subgroup $H = \langle \mu \rangle = \{\iota, \mu\}$ of the dihedral group $D_4 = \{\iota, \rho, \rho^2, \rho^3, \mu, \mu\rho, \mu\rho^2, \mu\rho^3\}$. Are the left cosets and right cosets of $H$ the same? Justify.6
14Show that if $H$ is a subgroup of index $2$ in a group $G$, then every left coset of $H$ is also a right coset of $H$. Hence prove that $g^{-1}hg \in H$ for all $g \in G$ and all $h \in H$.6
15Define the order of an element in a group $G$. Show that in a finite group $G$, the order of any element divides the order of $G$. Does every finite group contain an element of every order that divides the order of the group? Justify.6
16Show that every proper subgroup of a group $G$, with $o(G) = pq$ where $p$ and $q$ are prime numbers, is cyclic.6
17Suppose $H$ and $K$ are subgroups of a group $G$ such that $K \le H \le G$, and suppose $(H : K)$ and $(G : H)$ are both finite. Prove that $(G : K)$ is finite and $(G : K) = (G : H)(H : K)$.6
18(a) Show that a group with at least two elements but with no proper nontrivial subgroups must be finite and of prime order. (b) Using the theorem of Lagrange, show that if $n$ is odd, then an abelian group of order $2n$ contains at most one element of order $2$.6
