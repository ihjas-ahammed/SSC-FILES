Section A
All Questions can be answered. Each Question carries 3 marks (Ceiling: 24 Marks)
1 Let $f$ be continuous on $[a, b]$ such that $f(x) \ge 0$ for all $x \in [a, b]$. Prove that if $\int_a^b f = 0$, then $f(x) = 0$ for all $x \in [a, b]$. 3
2 Let $f \in \mathcal{R}[a, b]$ and let $c \in \mathbb{R}$. Define $g$ on $[a+c, b+c]$ by $g(y) = f(y-c)$. Prove that $g \in \mathcal{R}[a+c, b+c]$ and that $\int_{a+c}^{b+c} g = \int_a^b f$. 3
3 Define pointwise convergence of a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ on a subset $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Find the pointwise limit of $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x}{x+n}$ for $x \ge 0$. 3
4 Evaluate the pointwise limit of the sequence $f_n(x) = \dfrac{nx}{1+n^2 x^2}$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. 3
5 Define the uniform norm of a bounded function on a set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. Calculate the uniform norm $\|f_n - f\|_A$ for $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x}{n}$ on $A = [0, 1]$ where $f(x) = 0$. 3
6 State the Cauchy Criterion for the uniform convergence of a sequence of bounded functions on a set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$. 3
7 Give an example of a sequence of continuous functions on $[0, 1]$ whose pointwise limit is discontinuous. State where the uniform convergence fails. 3
8 Show by a concrete example that the limit of an integral is not necessarily equal to the integral of the limit, i.e., $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_a^b f_n(x)\,dx \ne \int_a^b \lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x)\,dx$. 3
9 State the theorem on the interchange of limit and continuity for a sequence of functions. Is uniform convergence a necessary condition for the continuity of the limit function? Justify. 3
10 Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of continuous functions on an interval $I$ converging uniformly on $I$ to $f$. If $(x_n) \subseteq I$ converges to $x_0 \in I$, show that $\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x_n) = f(x_0)$. 3
Section B
All Questions can be answered. Each Question carries 6 marks (Ceiling: 36 Marks)
11 Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous and suppose that $\int_0^x f = \int_x^1 f$ for all $x \in [0, 1]$. Prove that $f(x) = 0$ for all $x \in [0, 1]$. 6
12 Suppose $f$ is bounded by $M$ on $[a, b]$ and its restriction to $[c, b]$ is Riemann integrable for every $c \in (a, b)$. Using the Squeeze Theorem, prove that $f \in \mathcal{R}[a, b]$ and that $\lim_{c \to a^+} \int_c^b f = \int_a^b f$. 6
13 Let $f_n(x) = \dfrac{x^n}{1+x^n}$ for $x \ge 0$. Find the pointwise limit function $f(x)$ on $[0, \infty)$. Examine whether the convergence is uniform on $[0, 1]$ and on $[2, \infty)$. 6
14 Prove that a sequence $(f_n)$ of bounded functions on $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ converges uniformly on $A$ to a function $f$ if and only if $\|f_n - f\|_A \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$. 6
15 Let $f_n(x) = x + \dfrac{1}{n}$ and $f(x) = x$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Show that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $\mathbb{R}$, but the sequence of products $(f_n^2)$ does not converge uniformly to $f^2$ on $\mathbb{R}$. What does this demonstrate about the product of uniformly convergent sequences? 6
16 Let $f_n : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be defined for $n \ge 2$ by: $$f_n(x) = \begin{cases} n^2 x, & 0 \le x \le 1/n \\ -n^2(x - 2/n), & 1/n \le x \le 2/n \\ 0, & 2/n \le x \le 1 \end{cases}$$ Find the pointwise limit $f(x) = \lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x)$ on $[0, 1]$, and evaluate both $\lim_{n \to \infty}\int_0^1 f_n(x)\,dx$ and $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$. Explain why term-by-term integration fails in this case. 6
17 Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of continuous functions on a set $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ and suppose that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly on $A$ to a function $f$. Prove that $f$ is continuous on $A$. 6
18 State Dini's Theorem for uniform convergence of sequences of functions. Provide counterexamples showing that the theorem may fail if:
(a) The sequence is not monotone, or
(b) The underlying interval is not closed and bounded.		 6
