1. Basic mathematical equations

1.1 Inline and displayed

Put maths between single $ signs to keep it in the sentence: the area is A = πr2. Use \[ ... \] (or the equation* environment) for a centred display:

$A = \pi r^2$

\[
  \int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{3}.
\]
∫01 x2 dx = 13.

1.2 Numbered equations and labels

The equation environment numbers the formula. A \label lets you refer to it with \eqref:

\begin{equation}
  E = mc^2 \label{eq:einstein}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:einstein} is the mass--energy relation.
E = mc2 (1)

Equation (1) is the mass–energy relation.

1.3 Scripts, fractions and roots

x^{2}          x_{i}          x_{i}^{2}       % superscript, subscript
\frac{a+b}{c}  \sqrt{x}       \sqrt[3]{x}     % fraction, roots
\binom{n}{k}                                  % binomial coefficient

Rendered: x2, xi, xi2, (a+b)/c, √x, ∛x, C(n,k).

Braces group symbols. Without them, only the next character is affected: x^12 gives x12, while x^{12} gives x12.

2. Groups of equations and numbering

align lines up several equations at the & character. Each row ends with \\. align numbers every row; the starred version align* numbers none.

\begin{align}
  (a+b)^2 &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \label{eq:sq} \\
  (a-b)^2 &= a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \nonumber
\end{align}
(a+b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2 (2) (a−b)2 = a2 − 2ab + b2

Row (2) is numbered and the second row is not, because \nonumber suppresses its number. A single row with a custom label uses \tag{name}:

\begin{equation}
  \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 \tag{$\star$}
\end{equation}
sin2θ + cos2θ = 1 (★)

3. Matrices, dots, delimiters and affixed symbols

3.1 Matrices

Inside a display, the pmatrix (round brackets), bmatrix (square), vmatrix (determinant bars) and Bmatrix (curly braces) environments set matrices. Rows use \\ and columns use &.

\[
  A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 0 & -1 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 & 0 \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  \det\begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix} = ad - bc.
\]
A = 1   2   30  −1   45   6   0      det a  bc  d = ad − bc.

3.2 Dots

\[
  x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \qquad
  a_{11} + \cdots + a_{nn} \qquad
  \vdots \quad \ddots
\]

Use \ldots on the baseline, \cdots for centred dots between operators, \vdots and \ddots for vertical and diagonal dots.

x1, x2, …, xn    a11 + ⋅⋅⋅ + ann    ⋮   ⋱

3.3 Delimiters

Brackets that grow with their content use \left and \right:

\[
  \left( \frac{x}{y} \right)^{2} \qquad
  \left[ \sum_{k=1}^{n} k \right] \qquad
  \left\{ x \in \mathbb{R} : x > 0 \right\}
\]
(xy)2    [∑k=1n k]    { x ∈ ℝ : x > 0 }

Each \left needs a matching \right; use \right. when you want an invisible closing delimiter.

4. Operators, relations and symbols

4.1 Greek letters

Lowercase letters are the name with a backslash: \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \theta, \lambda, \pi, \sigma, \omega. Capital letters are \Gamma, \Delta, \Theta, \Lambda, \Sigma, \Omega.

Rendered: α, β, γ, θ, λ, π, σ, ω and Γ, Δ, Θ, Ω.

4.2 Big operators and limits

\[
  \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}, \qquad
  \prod_{i=1}^{n} i = n!, \qquad
  \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1
\]
∑n=1∞ 1/n2 = π2/6,    ∏i=1n i = n!,    limx→0 sin x / x = 1

4.3 Named functions

Use \sin, \cos, \log, \det and \max rather than italic letters. For a name that LaTeX does not provide, use \operatorname{name} (from amsmath): $\operatorname{sech}\,x = \dfrac{1}{\cosh x}$.

4.4 Relations, arrows and set notation

\[
  x \le y, \quad x \ne y, \quad x \approx y, \quad
  f\colon A \to B, \quad x \mapsto x^2
\]
\[
  a \in A, \quad A \subseteq B, \quad A \cup B, \quad A \cap B, \quad
  \forall\, \varepsilon > 0\ \exists\, \delta > 0
\]

5. A worked example combining the tools

Let A be a 2 × 2 matrix with eigenvalues λ1, λ2. Then

\begin{align*}
  \operatorname{tr}(A) &= \sum_{i=1}^{2} \lambda_i, \\
  \det(A)              &= \prod_{i=1}^{2} \lambda_i .
\end{align*}
tr(A) = ∑i=12 λi, det(A) = ∏i=12 λi.
