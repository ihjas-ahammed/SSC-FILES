## How to hook a question

1. Find the question's concept in the **Syllabus map** (Module I items 1–5).
2. Use the **Concept index** to get the book section, page and equation numbers.
3. Use the **Equation index** and **Examples** to check the formulas.
4. Use the **Problems index** for the book's own problems of the same type.
5. A section marked *beyond syllabus* is in the book but not in the Module 1 list. Use it only as background.
6. Questions from other modules have no entry here. Do not hook them to Module 1.
