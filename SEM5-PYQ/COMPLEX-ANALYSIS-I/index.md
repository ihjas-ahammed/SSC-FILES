# Complex Analysis I (MAT5CJ303, Sem 5): index

Sources (in this folder):
- `syllabus.pdf`: MAT5CJ303 Complex Analysis I syllabus (4 pages, copied from `SSC-V2/SEM5/MATH/`). Modules I–IV, plus Module V (open ended).
- `books/complex-analysis-textbook-module-N.pdf`: cuts from Zill and Shanahan, *Complex Analysis: A First Course with Applications* (3rd ed.), the syllabus textbook. Source: `SSC-FILES/2026 2/COMPLEX ANALYSIS/`.
  - Module I: Sec. 1.1–1.5 (printed pp. 3–33).
  - Module II: Sec. 2.1, 2.2 (printed pp. 47–61) and Sec. 2.4 (printed pp. 72–86).
  - Module III: Sec. 3.1–3.4 (printed pp. 103–140).
  - Module IV: Sec. 4.1 (printed pp. 151–165) and Sec. 4.3 (printed pp. 171–182).
- `pyq/`: undergraduate Complex Analysis question papers (see the PYQ table).

Page numbers are the book's printed numbers. The cut files keep printed page = PDF page − 14 within each range; the chapter opener pages are not included.

Left out of the cuts, because the syllabus puts them in Module V or does not list them: Sec. 2.3 (Linear Mappings), 2.5 (Reciprocal Function), 2.6, 3.5, 4.2 (Complex Powers), 4.4 (Inverse Trig and Hyperbolic) and 4.5. Module V is not extracted.
