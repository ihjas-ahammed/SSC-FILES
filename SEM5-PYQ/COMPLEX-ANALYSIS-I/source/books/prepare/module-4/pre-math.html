Pre-math bridge: Fraleigh, Module 4 (sections 16, 22 and 23)

Fraleigh, A First Course in Abstract Algebra (8th ed.), Section 16 (printed pp. 145–148), Section 22 (printed pp. 185–191), Section 23 (printed pp. 194–196). Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) mathematics. The syllabus numbers this material Module IV, items 17–22.

Module IV uses homomorphisms and kernels from Modules 2 and 3, and a few ideas from Plus Two and school arithmetic. The items marked Beyond are ideas to learn before reading.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two or school; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two.
