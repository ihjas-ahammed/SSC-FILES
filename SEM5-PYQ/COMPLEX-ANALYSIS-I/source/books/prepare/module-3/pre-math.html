Pre-math bridge: Fraleigh, Module 3 (sections 4, 8 and 10)

Fraleigh, A First Course in Abstract Algebra (8th ed.), Section 4 (printed pp. 43–46, items 4.8–4.15), Section 8 (printed pp. 82–84, items 8.19–8.25), Section 10 (printed pp. 97–104, items 10.1–10.20). Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) mathematics. The syllabus numbers this material Module III, items 13–16.

Module III uses the groups of Modules I and II and a few school and Plus Two ideas. The items marked Beyond are ideas to learn before reading.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two or school; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two. The Kerala Plus Two text has not been checked against this table; every status marked OPEN must be checked against it before the bridge is called final.
