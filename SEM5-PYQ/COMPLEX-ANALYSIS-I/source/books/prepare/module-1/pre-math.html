Pre-math bridge: Fraleigh, Module 1 (sections 1–2)

Fraleigh, A First Course in Abstract Algebra (8th ed.), Section 1, printed pp. 11–28. Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) mathematics. The syllabus numbers this material Module 1, items 1–6.

Section 1 uses little new mathematics. Most of it is number sets, arithmetic laws and functions, which Plus Two covers. The items marked Beyond are the ideas to learn before reading.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two; learn the basic idea first. The Kerala Plus Two text has not been checked against this table; statuses marked OPEN must be checked against it.
