Abstract Algebra I (MAT5CJ302): syllabus weightage vs actual weightage

Paper: Fifth Semester CUFYUGP, November 2026 (2024 admission onwards), 70 marks, 2 hours. Syllabus: CU-FYUGP MAT5CJ302, text Fraleigh 7th ed. Questions are placed in modules by the syllabus item their content belongs to (source traced in 2026-NOV-paper-trace.pdf).

Summary
  • The paper follows the syllabus closely: no module is more than 3.6 percentage points from its share of teaching hours.
  • Module I was over-weighted (28.6% of the marks on offer, against 25.0% of the hours). Module IV was under-weighted (21.4% against 25.0%).
  • Section C (10 marks) is all Module I and Module II. Modules III and IV only appear in Sections A and B.
  • Every module has at least 18 marks on offer in Sections A and B, above the syllabus minimum of 15 per module. Scaled to 70, Modules III and IV get exactly the minimum of 15.
  • Module V (open-ended) carries no marks in the syllabus, and no question came from it. Q19b is printed in the 8th ed. inside §12 (Factor Groups), but its content (an isomorphism of G with itself) is Module I.
