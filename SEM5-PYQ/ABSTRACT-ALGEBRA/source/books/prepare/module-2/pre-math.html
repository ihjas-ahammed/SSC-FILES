Pre-math bridge: Fraleigh, Module 2 (sections 5, 6 and 8)

Fraleigh, A First Course in Abstract Algebra (8th ed.), Section 5 (printed pp. 52–56), Section 6 (printed pp. 61–67), Section 8 (printed pp. 77–82, up to 8.18). Base: Kerala SCERT Plus Two (Class 12) mathematics. The syllabus numbers this material Module II, items 7–12.

Module II uses the group axioms from Module 1 and a few school and Plus Two ideas. The items marked Beyond are ideas to learn before reading. Only the items the notes use are listed.

Status key: Recall = taught in Plus Two or school; revise it. Partial = touched in Plus Two; the section needs more detail. Beyond = not in Plus Two; learn the basic idea first.
