How this paper works

A real paper has 12 short, 7 paragraph and 2 essay questions. This paper has twice as many (24 + 14 + 4), all from Module 4. Together they test every concept of the module; no concept is the main point of two questions.

Time4 hours (or two sittings of 2 hours) Maximum120 marks

PYQ N24 Q10 = asked in Nov 2024, question 10. If a concept was asked in several years, all of them are listed. "adapted" = a 1-mark fill-in or True/False turned into a short answer.

Section B · Paragraph / problemAnswer all questions in about half a page to one page. Each carries 5 marks. Ceiling 60.
25.
Derive Malus' law. Unpolarised light of intensity $I_0$ falls on three polaroids; the first and last are crossed and the middle one is at 45° to both. Find the emergent intensity.
26.
Derive Brewster's law, and show that at the polarising angle the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular.
27.
Explain the production of plane polarised light by a pile of plates. Why is the transmitted light, and not the reflected light, used?
28.
Calculate the thickness of a half wave plate and a quarter wave plate of quartz for a wavelength of 5000 Å. Here µE is 1.553 and µO is 1.544.
PYQ N20 Q28 also N19 Q30 · N22 Q30 · N25 Q17
29.
Velocity of E-ray along the optic axis of a calcite crystal is $1.81\times10^8$ m s⁻¹ and its velocity perpendicular to the optic axis is $2.02\times10^8$ m s⁻¹. Find the velocity of E-ray along an axis that is at 45° angle with the optic axis.
PYQ N25 Q16
30.
Using Huygens' construction, show that when the optic axis is perpendicular to the plane of incidence, the E-ray obeys Snell's law with refractive index $\mu_e$.
31.
Describe the construction and working of a Nicol prism.
32.
A wave plate is placed between crossed polarisers with its optic axis at angle φ to the first polariser. Show that $I = I_1\sin^2 2\phi\,\sin^2(\delta/2)$. Find the intensity (in terms of the incident unpolarised $I_0$) for a QWP and for a HWP with φ = 45°.
33.
Left circularly polarised light ($\lambda = 5893$ Å) falls normally on a calcite plate of thickness 0.005141 mm cut with its optic axis parallel to its faces ($\mu_o - \mu_e = 0.17195$). Find the phase difference introduced and state the nature of the emergent light.
34.
Show that plane polarised light can be regarded as the sum of right and left circularly polarised light, and hence explain Fresnel's theory of optical rotation. Find the rotation produced by 1 mm of quartz along its optic axis if $|n_L - n_R| = 7\times10^{-5}$ and $\lambda = 6000$ Å.
35.
Explain the method of producing plane, circularly and elliptically polarized light.
PYQ N20 Q23
36.
Define double refraction. Explain Laurent's half-shade polarimeter.
PYQ N24 Q18
37.
A calcite quarter-wave plate is made for λ = 4046 Å ($\mu_o-\mu_e = 0.18440$). Find its thickness. If it is used with λ = 7065 Å ($\mu_o-\mu_e = 0.16848$) and plane polarised light at 45° to its axis, what light emerges?
38.
What is partially polarised light? When an analyser is rotated in a beam, the maximum intensity is three times the minimum. Find the degree of polarisation and describe the beam.

PYQ N20 Q34 (first two parts)
41.
Explain double refraction on the basis of Huygens' theory. Draw the wave surfaces for negative and positive uniaxial crystals, and use Huygens' construction for normal incidence when the optic axis is (a) parallel to the surface, (b) perpendicular to the surface, (c) inclined to the surface.
42.
Obtain the resultant of two perpendicular simple harmonic vibrations of the same frequency with a phase difference δ. Discuss the cases δ = 0, π/2, π and general δ, and explain how these give plane, circular and elliptical polarisation.
