Which tool?

Mixed drill. Name the tool first, then check its conditions. Answers below.

  1. Find $\lim_n f_n(x)$.
  2. Show $f_n \to f$ uniformly, and $|f_n(x) - f(x)| \le$ something with no x.
  3. Show uniform, but the gap depends on x.
  4. Show not uniform; the $f_n$ are continuous and the limit jumps.
  5. Show not uniform; the limit is continuous.
  6. Show uniform convergence without knowing f.
  7. Prove a uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous.
  8. Show $\lim \int f_n \ne \int \lim f_n$.
Answers
1. Fix x, cases, P1–P2 (T2). 2. Bound free of x (T8). 3. Norm by calculus, Lemma 8.1.8 (T6, T7). 4. Theorem 8.2.2 contrapositive (T14). 5. Points $x_k$ with gap ≥ ε₀ (Lemma 8.1.5, T4), or norm not → 0. 6. Cauchy criterion 8.1.10 (T10). 7. ε/3 argument (T13). 8. Compute both sides (T12).

Theorem sheet

8.1.1 Pointwisefor each $x \in A_0$, $f_n(x) \to f(x)$; K may depend on ε and x.
8.1.4 Uniformfor each ε there is K(ε), not depending on x, with $|f_n(x) - f(x)| < \varepsilon$ for all $n \ge K$ and all $x \in A_0$.
8.1.5 Not uniform⇔ some $\varepsilon_0>0$, subsequence $f_{n_k}$, points $x_k$ with $|f_{n_k}(x_k) - f(x_k)| \ge \varepsilon_0$.
8.1.7 Uniform norm$\lVert\varphi\rVert_A = \sup\{|\varphi(x)| : x \in A\}$ for bounded φ.
8.1.8 Norm testbounded $f_n$: uniform on A ⇔ $\lVert f_n - f\rVert_A \to 0$.
8.1.10 Cauchy criterionbounded $f_n$: uniform to a bounded f ⇔ ∀ε ∃H: $\lVert f_m - f_n\rVert_A \le \varepsilon$ for $m, n \ge H$.
8.2.2 Continuity$f_n$ continuous on A, $f_n \to f$ uniformly on A ⇒ f continuous on A.

About 4 minutes. 6 marksPointwise limit, then the gap, then its sup (or $x_k$), then the verdict with the theorem named. 8–12 lines. 10 marksStatement word for word, Know/Want, numbered proof, then "where each hypothesis was used". If stuck, still write the skeleton.
