MODULE II: MODEL ANSWERS
For checking your work. Each answer shows the key step and the result.

Section A. 1. $u_k(x)$ is periodic with the lattice period, and $\psi(x)=e^{ikx}u_k(x)$ (book Eq. 6.4). 2. $f(E)=1$ for $EE_{F0}$ (book Eq. 5.29). 3. $v_g=\dfrac{1}{\hbar}\dfrac{dE}{dk}$ (book Eq. 6.49). 4. $2N$ electrons (two spins in each of the $N$ $k$-states of the band; book §6.3.2).

Section B. 5. $N=\dfrac{V}{3\pi^2}\left(\dfrac{2mE_F}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}$ (book Eq. 5.37). 6. $T_F=E_F/k_B$ (book §5.3.1). 7. $E_{F0}$ and $E_0$ are related by $E_0=\tfrac35E_{F0}$ (book §5.2.2). 8. $E_n=n^2h^2/8mL^2$; the top level $n_F=N/2$ gives $E_F=\dfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(\dfrac{N\pi}{2L}\right)^2$ (book Eqs. 5.10, 5.12, 5.13).

Section C. 9. Count the $k$-states in a sphere of radius $k$: $2\cdot\tfrac43\pi k^3/(2\pi/L)^3=\dfrac{L^3}{3\pi^2}k^3=N$. With $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar$ and $N(E)=\dfrac{V}{3\pi^2}\left(\dfrac{2mE}{\hbar^2}\right)^{3/2}$, differentiate: $D(E)=dN/dE$ gives Eq. 5.40. 10. At $E=E_F$, $f=1/[e^0+1]=\tfrac12$ for any $T$. At $T>0$ electrons within about $k_BT$ of $E_F$ can jump to empty states; those deeper cannot (book §5.2.2). 11. $a=\dfrac{e\mathcal E}{\hbar^2}\dfrac{d^2E}{dk^2}$ (book Eq. 6.53); comparing with $a=e\mathcal E/m$ gives $m^*=\hbar^2/(d^2E/dk^2)$ (Eq. 6.55). 12. $d^2E/dk^2=E_0a^2\cos ka$, which is $E_0a^2$ at $k=0$. So $m^*=\hbar^2/(E_0a^2)$. 13. At a completely filled band $k_1=\pi/a$ and $dE/dk=0$ there, so $N_{\text{eff}}=0$ (book Eq. 6.59). $N_{\text{eff}}$ is largest when $dE/dk$ is largest, at the inflexion point. 14. Condition (6.39). $P\to0$: $\cos\alpha a=\cos ka$, so $\alpha=k$, the free electron case with all energies allowed. $P\to\infty$: $\sin\alpha a=0$, so $\alpha a=\pm n\pi$, which gives levels (book §6.3).

Section D. 15. $E_{F0}=\dfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}(3\pi^2 n)^{2/3}$ (5.47) from $N=\int_0^{E_{F0}}D\,dE$; $E_0=\tfrac1N\int_0^{E_{F0}}E\,D\,dE=\tfrac35E_{F0}$; $D(E)$ as Eq. (5.40). 16. Bloch form and the Wronskian give $\lambda^2-(\alpha_1+\beta_2)\lambda+1=0$; in the delta limit (6.37) with $P$ (6.38), giving Eq. (6.39). Allowed bands where the left side lies in $[-1,1]$; forbidden otherwise (Fig. 6.3). Limits as C14. 17. Metal: partly filled band, $N_{\text{eff}}\neq0$. Insulator: full bands with a wide gap (diamond about 6 eV). Intrinsic semiconductor: full band with a narrow gap (about 1 eV), conduction by thermal excitation. Full band: $N_{\text{eff}}=0$ (6.59); maximum at the inflexion point.
