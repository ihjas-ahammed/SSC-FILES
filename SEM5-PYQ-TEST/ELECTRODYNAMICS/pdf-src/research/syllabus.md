# Electrodynamics II — syllabus extraction

Source: `SSC-V2/SEM5/PHY/PHY4CJ203_ELECTRODYNAMICS_II.pdf` (Calicut University FYUGP BSc Physics Honours Syllabus 2024, printed pages 95–100 onward). Course code PHY4CJ203, Semester V, Core in Major, 4 credits, 3 lecture + 2 practical hours/week, 75 hours total. Prerequisite is Electrodynamics I.

## Official theory-module boundaries and weight

| Module | Official title | Hours | Marks | Topics / syllabus units |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| I | Electric and Magnetic Fields in Matter | 12 | 16 | 1 Polarization (2h); 2 Field of a Polarised Object (2h); 3 Electric Displacement, boundary conditions, susceptibility, permittivity and dielectric constant of linear dielectrics (3h); 4 Magnetisation (2h); 5 Field of a Magnetised Object; physical interpretation of bound currents (physical concept only); Ampère’s law in magnetized materials; magnetic susceptibility and permeability; ferromagnetism (3h). |
| II | Electrodynamics | 12 | 18 | 6 Ohm’s law, electromotive force, motional emf (3h); 7 electromagnetic induction: Faraday’s law, induced electric field, inductance, energy in magnetic fields (4h); 8 electrodynamics before Maxwell, Maxwell’s correction to Ampère’s law, Maxwell’s equations, equations in matter, boundary conditions (5h). |
| III | Electromagnetic Waves | 8 | 18 | 9 waves in one dimension, sinusoidal waves, polarization (2h); 10 wave equations for E and B (1h); 11 monochromatic plane waves (1h); 12 Poynting’s theorem (2h); 13 energy and momentum in EM waves (1h); 14 propagation in linear media (1h). |
| IV | Transient Circuits and Alternating Currents | 13 | 18 | 15 growth of current in series L-R, C-R and L-C circuits (relevant portions) (2h); 16 decay of current in L-R, C-R and L-C circuits (relevant portions) (2h); 17 AC emf in rotating coil in magnetic field (2h); 18 AC circuit with R only, L only, C only (2h); 19 use of j operator in AC (relevant concepts) (1h); 20 AC circuit with L and R, C and R, parallel L and C (2h); 21 series LCR circuit (1h); 22 power in AC (1h). |

These are the four theory modules (45 hours, 70 marks). Module V is practicals (30 hours, 30 marks), outside the four-module theory kit.

## Exam pattern evidence

It does not itself specify the written-paper question-section format. Use actual current-syllabus question papers for that pattern; do not infer section counts from this syllabus.

## Scope notes

- Unit 5 explicitly limits bound-current physical interpretation to the physical concept.
- Units 15, 16 and 19 explicitly qualify coverage as “relevant portions” / “relevant concepts.”
- Ch. 7 §7.3.4 (magnetic charge) is omitted from the Module II prescribed reading list; topics outside the listed syllabus units/sections require explicit marking as legacy/out-of-scope if present in older papers.
