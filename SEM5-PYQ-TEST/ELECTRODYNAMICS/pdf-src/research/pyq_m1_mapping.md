# Module I mapping for legacy Electrodynamics I questions

The six 2025 CBCSS questions below are useful as legacy practice because their concepts are explicitly present in the Electrodynamics II Module I content in `content_m1.py`. They are not target-course PYQs. Prefer preserving the sourced prompts and label them as PYQ practice; only B18 needs a scope cue to keep the requested response conceptual.

| Source item | Source prompt | Exact fit / adaptation recommendation | Module I support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 A3 (2 marks) | Define magnetic susceptibility. | Exact short-answer fit. If this kit expects complete conceptual framing, append “and state how it relates M and H for a linear material.” That addition is an adaptation, so retain the original as the PYQ prompt. | T13 magnetic-material classification; T11/H material relation. |
| 2025 A5 (2 marks) | Define atomic polarizability. | Exact short-answer fit. For a slightly more useful practice variant, ask for the weak-field relation between induced dipole moment and local field, $p=\alpha E_{local}$; list this separately as an adaptation. | T1 dielectrics and induced dipoles. |
| 2025 A9 (2 marks) | Distinguish paramagnets and diamagnets. | Exact fit; answer should compare sign/origin of susceptibility and direction of response. Avoid adding ferromagnets unless explicitly marked as an extended variant. | T13 magnetic-material classification; T8 qualitative atomic response. |
| 2025 A10 (2 marks) | What is the physical interpretation of bound charges. | Exact fit. To make the target convention explicit, an optional adapted prompt is “Explain how polarization leaves bound surface charge and, when nonuniform, bound volume charge.” | T4 bound charge and field of polarization; T2 microscopic cancellation. |
| 2025 B17 (5 marks) | Show that the potential of a polarized object is the same as that produced by a volume charge density plus a surface charge density. | Exact, strong fit. It directly tests the prescribed derivation from dipole potential, integration by parts, and identification of $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot P$, $\sigma_b=P\cdot n$. Keep the original text; no adaptation needed. | Advanced T4 derivation of equivalent bound charges; foundational T3 field/potential; T2. |
| 2025 B18 (5 marks) | Discuss briefly about the bound currents in a magnetized medium. | Fit with a scope cue. Preserve the prompt but tell students to discuss loop cancellation and identify surface/bulk bound currents physically; the official syllabus says bound-current interpretation is “physical concept only.” Do not expect a full derivation of $J_b=\nabla\times M$ and $K_b=M\times n$ as required exam work. | T10 bound currents and magnetized objects; T14 uniform-M consequence. |

All six belong in the Module I legacy-practice set. Module attribution should be `I`; `source_year` 2025; `source_paper` Electrodynamics I (Semester III CBCSS, 2020–2023 admissions). Do not label them as PYQs from Electrodynamics II.
