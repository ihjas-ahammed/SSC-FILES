## Corrections to the initial collection pass

- N25 Q19 belongs directly to electric displacement in Module I.
- N25 Q11 is a definition used by Griffiths §6.2.1 for the field of a magnetized object.
- N25 Q21 is included with continuity and Maxwell’s repair in Module II.
- A26 Q10 (loop torque) overlaps magnetic dipoles; A26 Q15 overlaps the magnetic Maxwell flux law.
- A26 Q16 is expanded from an air solenoid to a linear magnetic core, and the answer also explicitly gives the original air-core inside/outside result.
- N25 Q6 is included as the magnetostatic limit in the integral Maxwell system.
- A26 Q7 says magnetic force cannot “accelerate” a particle. Taken literally that is false: it can change velocity direction while leaving speed and kinetic energy unchanged. This ambiguous prerequisite question is not reproduced in the model papers.
