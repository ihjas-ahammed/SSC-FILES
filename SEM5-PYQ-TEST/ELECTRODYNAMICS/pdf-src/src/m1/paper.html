Practice instructions

Suggested time: two sittings of two hours. Section A: 20 questions × 3 marks, ceiling 48. Section B: 16 × 6, ceiling 72. Section C: answer any two of four × 10. Maximum: 140.

This doubles the supplied April 2026 Electrodynamics I 70-mark practice pattern. The current ED II syllabus specifies 70 theory marks but does not supply a question pattern here. This is a study paper, not an official FYUGP mock.

Each numbered question has a distinct primary assessment target. Longer questions combine earlier methods. N25 means November 2025 CBCSS ED I; A26 means April 2026 FYUGP ED I. Tags explicitly identify these older or prerequisite courses. Expanded/adapted means wording, scope or mark allocation was changed. Overlapping past questions are tagged with their original course and number; untagged questions are original model practice.

All four theory modules are included in the set. Module V is laboratory work and is outside this theory-paper series.
